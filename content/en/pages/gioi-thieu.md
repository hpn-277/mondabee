## MOTHERNATURE

**The spark of an idea**

Mother Nature grew out of a small household beekeeping operation. Seeing that honey was a genuinely good product for people's health, yet still under-appreciated in the domestic market, we built the Mother Nature honey brand with the goal of making honey more widely and commonly used at home.

Beyond our pure coffee-flower honey, we have since launched other honey-based products as well.

**_For the health of the community – Mother Nature was created for you._**

**_Vision: To become the leading company in processing honey products in Vietnam._**

**MOTHERNATURE'S PRODUCTS**

You have many choices in life — let **Mother Nature** be the choice for your family's health.

#### Vision
![](/images/pages/gioi-thieu/abi1.png)

To be the pioneer in processing honey combined with natural herbs in the Vietnamese market, and to bring a Vietnamese honey brand to the international stage.

#### Mission
![](/images/pages/gioi-thieu/abi2.png)

Improving life expectancy for Vietnamese people through natural herbs, and raising the value of Vietnamese agricultural produce on the international market.

#### Core Values
![](/images/pages/gioi-thieu/abi3.png)

Discipline – Fairness – Improvement – Sharing

Working with discipline is our first guiding principle.

Fair value for everyone involved (good purchase prices for farmers, quality products for consumers, balanced benefit for producers and distributors).

Constant improvement and creativity in our work, to maximize value for customers and society.

Sharing with those in need: people in difficult circumstances often lack access to healthcare, so we want to use our position as a producer, and a share of our profit, to help them access good, natural food for their health too.

#### Company Culture
![](/images/pages/gioi-thieu/abi4.png)

**Freedom – Independence – Self-reliance – Self-respect – Resilience**

We always try to live fully by these five values!

#### Logo Meaning
![](/images/pages/gioi-thieu/logo-final-1-1.png)

Kim Truc Plus chose the brand name Mother Nature because we want to bring the precious gifts that mother nature generously offers closer to every Vietnamese family.

Alongside that, our brand logo was designed around a graceful bee, meant to celebrate the beauty of women. We hope women always love themselves, build their own livelihoods with confidence, and keep the values of family close.
