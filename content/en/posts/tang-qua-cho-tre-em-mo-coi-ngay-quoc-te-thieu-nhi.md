On International Children's Day (June 1), 2022, Mother Nature was honored to contribute small gifts to orphaned children in Thu Duc city. With the motto "Giving lasts forever," Mother Nature hopes to spread positive values to the community and draw more attention and care to the disadvantaged children there.

## What Is International Children's Day?

International Children's Day, also sometimes called the Children's Festival, is a holiday dedicated to children all around the world, not just in Vietnam. The holiday takes place on June 1 each year in more than 30 countries. Throughout International Children's Day, children receive loving wishes from their parents, families, and relatives, as well as special gifts they love. It could be said that Children's Day is an occasion for parents to express their love for their children.

## The Meaning of International Children's Day

It could be said that International Children's Day holds a particularly important meaning for organizations and associations working to care for and protect children. International Children's Day is an opportunity to help children access their basic legal rights, thereby raising the awareness and understanding of individuals and groups in protecting children's interests and their rights to life, education, and development.

Speeches on children's rights and welfare, television programs with content suitable for children, and other activities marking International Children's Day are taking place around the world.

## Gift-Giving Activities on International Children's Day

On June 1st, in celebration of the children's holiday, Mother Nature took part in a charity program organized by the GOLF Hanoi Saigon Club. [The GOLF Hanoi Saigon Club](https://www.facebook.com/clbgolfhnsg/) called for donations, however small, from generous benefactors across the country. With the motto "the whole leaf wraps around the torn one" (helping those less fortunate), gifts were delivered directly to orphaned children at Dieu Giac Pagoda in Thu Duc city. The program received support and contributions from many generous benefactors from all over the country. Gifts such as rice, milk, cookies, and laundry detergent were given to the children - items that are truly essential in their daily lives.

## Closing Words from Mother Nature on the Occasion of International Children's Day

For [Mother Nature](https://monabee.vn/), seeing a smile bloom on the children's faces is a huge motivation for Mother Nature to continue growing in the future. The direction and values that Mother Nature brings to the community are not limited to personal gains and benefits, but above all, follow the motto "Giving lasts forever." This motto is the guiding compass for all of Mother Nature's activities. Though the gifts given to the children may not be much, above all, Mother Nature wants to take part in charitable activities that benefit the community, spreading compassion for unfortunate individuals facing hardship. Like the butterfly effect, this will spread even more strongly among individuals in society. Having gifts to give is a way of supporting those who care for children, and above all, helping the children find joy on their own special day. Visiting and caring for them represents tremendous and precious emotional value. There's no need to wait until you're wealthy - whenever society needs it, Mother Nature always has something to give.
