Honey is a natural product that's very good for health. However, not everyone knows the proper way to store honey. Today, [**Mother Nature Honey**](https://monabee.vn/) will guide you on **how to store honey** to protect your health.

![How to store honey](http://honeco.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/doi-nuoi-ong-thu-hoach-mat-ong-1024x684.jpg)

## **How long does honey keep?**

**Honey** is very good for health—it's not only a remedy for the elderly and children, and a seasoning for cooking, but also an ingredient for beauty care, and it's purchased in large quantities. However, if not stored properly, honey's quality will decline.

For Mother Nature Honey's products, the honey is cared for through a clean beekeeping process from raw material to finished product, and it's carefully stored in glass jars, keeping for up to 5 years from the production date.

## **Guide to storing honey for a long time**

Let's look at the **proper way to store honey** so it can be used year-round without losing its effectiveness.

## **Choosing a container to store honey**

In the past, before science and technology had developed, ceramic jars were the only choice for storing honey. Using ceramics to store honey is very safe, and your honey jar also carries a traditional beauty. However, you need to be very careful with ceramics, as they can easily break if bumped.

Besides ceramics, glass—made from sand—is completely clean, colorless, odorless, and environmentally friendly. Storing honey in a glass jar is the safest, most ideal option. Mother Nature Honey's higher-value honey products are all stored in glass jars. However, glass bottles have the downside of being fragile, so keep them in a safe, enclosed place.

![](https://monabee.vn/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/mat-ong-mother-nature-5-1-300x183.jpg)Storing honey in a glass jar

Besides glass jars, you can also store honey in plastic bottles/jars/containers, as long as they meet food safety standards, are clean, colorless, and odorless. Mother Nature Honey's products meant for cooking are prioritized to be stored in plastic bottles, since they're easier to transport, unbreakable, and still safe for the honey.

![](https://monabee.vn/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/mat-ong-mother-nature-3-300x176.jpg)Storing honey in a plastic bottle

In Mother Nature Honey's own view, if you're using the honey as a gift or for health, glass or ceramic jars should be your top choice, while for cooking purposes, plastic bottles are more convenient to use.

### **Ideal environmental conditions for storing honey**

To keep honey's quality from declining, store it at room temperature, ideally around 20–25°C.

Keep honey in a dry, well-ventilated place away from direct sunlight. You can store it on a kitchen shelf, kitchen cabinet, or dry food pantry—never store honey in direct sunlight, as this will reduce its quality.

### **Seal the container tightly after use**

To keep honey's flavor from being affected by outside air, minimize its exposure as much as possible—after use, make sure the honey bottle/jar/container is tightly sealed.

### **What to avoid when storing honey**

**Can honey be stored in metal containers or utensils?** Honey contains many organic acids and sugars, and when these substances come into contact with metal, an oxidation process occurs that alters honey's nutrient content. Storing honey in metal containers can contaminate it, which is extremely dangerous.

### **How to tell if honey's quality has declined**

After honey has been used for a while, even if it's still within its shelf life, if it shows the following unusual signs, you should throw it away immediately!

Color: Honey comes in many colors (yellow, amber-brown, black, etc.); if honey becomes increasingly darker than its original color, its quality has declined.

Taste: If, when tasted, it no longer has its original sweetness but instead tastes sour or spicy, or forms bubbles, your jar of honey may have fermented.
