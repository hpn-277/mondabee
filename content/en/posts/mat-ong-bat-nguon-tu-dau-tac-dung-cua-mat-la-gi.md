Honey is a highly valuable gift given to humans by nature, gathered by bees from flowers found in nature. Honey is an unfermented sweet substance produced when bees collect nectar from flowers or secretions from other parts of plants. After gathering and processing the nectar, the honey is stored daily in the honeycomb of the hive.

For [honey](https://hitavegan.com/tac-dung-cua-mat-ong/), it's especially important that it contain no unpleasant odors, chemical tastes, or harmful substances. Honey is an extremely beneficial product for humans, and comes in 2 forms — "farmed honey and wild forest honey" — but both are pure honey produced by bees.

### **2. [The origin of honey](https://vi.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%E1%BA%ADt_ong)**

As for [the origin of honey](https://hitavegan.com/tac-dung-cua-mat-ong/), it remains unclear to this day, but based on historical records, honey has been used across many fields throughout much of history. During that era, as humans were gradually evolving and beginning to hunt and live in forests, people discovered that honey tasted sweet and had no adverse effects on their health.

#### **a. [How is honey farmed](https://hitavegan.com/tac-dung-cua-mat-ong/)?**

Today, most people use 2 breeds of bees — "super-honey bees and super-laying bees." Bees are cared for and protected by humans, who maintain their populations and bring many benefits to local farmers, helping crops grow better and providing an essential source of income.

Bees are kept in a hive box, in locations with plenty of nectar and pollen. They're cared for with a suitable diet to ensure full nutrition and keep them healthy. A single bee's lifespan, from hatching to death, is about one month, which is why humans need to maintain the population so bees don't die out.

#### **b. What products come from honey?**

Honey-related products include "Honey, Pollen, Royal Jelly, and Beeswax" — a diverse range of products that are mostly beneficial for people, both helping economic development and bringing many benefits to daily life.

## _**Not only that, the benefits of honey also help women stay healthy and beautiful, with radiant, glowing skin**_

### **![](/images/posts/mat-ong-bat-nguon-tu-dau-tac-dung-cua-mat-la-gi/11.jpg)**

### **1. Honey's benefits for hair and scalp care**

[Honey](https://hitavegan.com/toi-ngam-mat-ong/) helps prevent dandruff, fungal infections, and scalp inflammation, and also helps your hair become smoother and shinier. If you're experiencing hair loss, honey is also a smart choice for your family, as it protects hair follicles, prevents weak, thinning hair, and stimulates faster hair growth. The trick is extremely simple — just mix honey with water in a 1:3 ratio and rub it evenly onto your hair, leave it on for 30 minutes, then wash it out with shampoo. You can also mix honey with coconut oil to boost the effect for your hair.

### **2. Honey's benefits for preventing acne**

Rich in [antioxidants](https://hitavegan.com/chat-chong-oxy-hoa/) and possessing mild antibacterial properties, honey also has a positive effect on skin areas inflamed or reddened by acne. Mixing honey with [turmeric powder](https://hitavegan.com/tac-dung-cua-mat-ong/) and applying it to a pimple helps reduce swelling and pain, helps the pimple dry out and shrink faster, and helps heal skin darkened by acne scars.

### **3. Honey's benefits for beautiful, radiant skin**

The vitamin B and C in honey help nourish skin, making it healthier, more youthful, and smoother. That's why honey appears in so many masks and moisturizers on the market. You can apply masks such as [honey-lemon](https://hitavegan.com/mat-ong-bac-ha/), honey-aloe vera, honey-avocado, or honey-egg 2 to 3 times a week to improve your skin.

 

 

### **4. Honey's benefits for healing scars and wounds**

[Antibacterial ability](https://hitavegan.com/gung-mat-ong/) is one of honey's most effective properties. Many topical skin medications contain honey, which is very helpful in preventing inflammation in external wounds. As a result, wounds heal faster, with less darkening and less scarring left behind.

### **5. Honey's benefits for boosting memory**

Honey, rich in [antioxidants](https://hitavegan.com/chat-chong-oxy-hoa/), can help prevent damage and cell loss in the brain. A 2011 study published in Menopause found that consuming a full spoon of Malaysian honey every day helped boost memory in menopausal women. Additionally, honey helps the body better absorb calcium, a nutrient essential for thinking and decision-making in the brain.
