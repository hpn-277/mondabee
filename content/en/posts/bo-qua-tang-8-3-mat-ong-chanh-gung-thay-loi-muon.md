## What is March 8th?

March 8th is [International Women's Day](https://vi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ng%C3%A0y_Qu%E1%BB%91c_t%E1%BA%BF_Ph%E1%BB%A5_n%E1%BB%AF). This holiday is celebrated not only in Vietnam but also in many other countries around the world. On this day, men take the opportunity to give the women in their lives—mothers, girlfriends—their most loving and heartfelt wishes. The origin of this holiday traces back to the struggles of female workers whose efforts were undervalued and paid unfairly low wages compared to the work they put in. Although the movement was brutally suppressed not long after, it became a rallying cry that echoed around the world. On March 8, 1917, the Soviet Union declared this day a national holiday, which later spread to other socialist countries.
