## What is a Typical Rural Industrial Product?

Since 2014, the Ministry of Industry and Trade has run a program to select typical rural industrial products for small and medium enterprises and cooperatives. This is a large-scale program held every two years, starting in 2014, and has now been run 5 times. To kick off this program, the [Ba Ria - Vung Tau](http://knkn.baria-vungtau.gov.vn/) provincial People's Committee recently rolled out a plan to honor rural industrial products of high quality, sustainable use value, and future development potential. Since then, the province has selected more than 80 products, of which 30 were selected at the regional level and 10 advanced to the national-level selection round.

## Benefits of Being a Typical Rural Industrial Product

For each product selected as a typical rural industrial product, this recognition serves as a passport helping the product gain stronger access to the market. According to Ms. Cao Thi Hong Van, director of Hoa Long Mushroom Farming Co., Ltd. in Ba Ria City, once a product wins the title of typical rural industrial product, businesses gain more confidence in it. Their product becomes known to more people and its quality is recognized. This is also a great opportunity to promote the product's image.

Beyond that, a product certified as a typical rural industrial product will also enjoy a number of other specific benefits, such as:

  * Being permitted to print and affix the logo of the typical rural industrial product program on the product or its packaging
  * Being provided with information and guidance on accessing state incentive programs
  * Being given priority to receive support from local and national industrial promotion funding, and being considered for building technology-transfer demonstration models and applying advanced machinery, equipment, and scientific and technical advances to production  
And many other incentives besides

## Mother Nature - A Typical Rural Industrial Product of Ba Ria - Vung Tau

Kim Truc Plus Co., Ltd., originally a beekeeping farm operating since 1995 in Xuyen Moc district, Ba Ria - Vung Tau province, has specialized in supplying export-standard honey to companies in Ho Chi Minh City and Dong Nai. Building on its existing strengths and grasping market demand, Kim Truc Plus Co., Ltd. launched the honey brand **"Mother Nature"** , with the slogan _"A gift from Mother Nature"_ , specializing in researching and processing products from pure honey combined with natural herbs to boost the nutritional and medicinal value of the products.

Kim Truc Plus's products are newly launched on the market but have already been widely embraced by consumers. In particular, 2 products — Mother Nature lemon ginger honey and Mother Nature turmeric royal jelly honey — have achieved certification as Typical Rural Industrial Products at the provincial and regional level for 2020. Furthermore, Mother Nature hopes to export to the world its lines of honey-based products combined with other foods of high nutritional and medicinal value from Vietnam, so that the world knows Vietnam offers more than just raw honey and raw agricultural produce.

## Mother Nature's Production Process

To achieve certification as a typical rural industrial product, Mother Nature always ensures it meets the following criteria:

  * Be a product manufactured by the rural industrial facility itself, without infringing on intellectual property or industrial property rights
  * Meet regulations and standards on occupational hygiene and safety, food safety, and environmental protection
  * Have the capacity to produce in large quantities to meet market demand



Mother Nature's products meet testing standards for both input and output materials; its processing facilities meet regulatory food safety and hygiene standards in order to bring to market nutritious, convenient, and safe processed honey products.

With modern machinery and equipment and a scientific workflow, Mother Nature fully deserves the title of regional-level typical rural industrial product for 2020.
