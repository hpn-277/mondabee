## Who is Mother Nature?

A honey brand founded not long ago, yet it has already won the support of many customers across the country. Mother Nature grew out of a beekeeping household that recognized how essential honey is for health, while noticing that awareness of it among Vietnamese people was still low. So the Mother Nature honey brand was born to bring users a fresh perspective on honey, helping honey blended with herbs such as lemon, ginger, ginseng, and turmeric starch become more widely and popularly used.

"For public health – Mother Nature was born for you"

With the vision of becoming a leading company in Vietnam's honey processing industry, Mother Nature keeps developing and researching new product lines. Beyond that, Mother Nature also sets aside a significant portion of its revenue to support individuals and families in difficult circumstances, giving them the chance to use Mother Nature's products.

## Products currently available at Mother Nature

Mother Nature doesn't stop at simply providing pure natural honey harvested at full ripeness from bee gardens in [Lam Dong](https://danviet.vn/lam-dong-nuoi-ong-mat-an-toan-sinh-hoc-trong-vuon-ca-phe-vua-ban-sua-vua-ban-mat-nhieu-nguoi-keo-den-xem-20210816161315808.htm). Mother Nature also develops related products by combining honey with various herbs.

  * Honey with lemon and ginger
  * Honey with ginseng and turmeric starch
  * Honey with natural royal jelly

Combining pure honey with herbs not only makes the flavor richer and more delicious but also boosts the medicinal effectiveness, improving users' health in line with Mother Nature's guiding principle of always putting customers' health first.

All ingredients at Mother Nature have clear origins and are strictly tested by relevant authorities and agencies. Customers can use these products with complete peace of mind.

## Mother Nature always stands by consumers

"Living means giving, not only receiving" — Mother Nature periodically sets aside a significant portion of its revenue for community activities. At Mother Nature, we believe every individual deserves love and deserves to receive the best from the community where they live, study, and work. Understanding this, Mother Nature has carried out practical activities such as:

  * Donating 350 bottles of honey with lemon and ginger to frontline doctors fighting the pandemic in Ho Chi Minh City
  * The "Warm Winter Night" program, giving 100 gifts to the poor and homeless on New Year's Eve
  * And other annual activities

Mother Nature feels truly happy to receive the support and trust of customers, and to join them in spreading positive values to individuals facing more difficult circumstances.
