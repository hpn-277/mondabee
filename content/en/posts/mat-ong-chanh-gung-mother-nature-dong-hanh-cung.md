> #### Currently, the Covid-19 virus pandemic is a disease capable of transmitting from animals to humans. To fight it, we need a truly healthy body. Besides eating, resting properly, and exercising every day, we also need to supplement our diet with certain functional foods that benefit health.

#### **Honey**, **ginger**, and **lemon** have long been known as natural, nutrient-rich foods with antibacterial properties that are good for health: honey has been researched and proven to contain many antioxidants that help boost the immune system. Ginger, a staple ingredient in most Vietnamese kitchens, helps keep the body warm, is good for digestion, and is good for the immune system due to its antibacterial properties and its ability to fight natural parasites. Lemon helps supply the body with nutrients like vitamin C. Adding lemon is essential to help reduce the "heaty" nature of honey and ginger.

#### ![](/images/posts/mat-ong-chanh-gung-mother-nature-dong-hanh-cung/matchanhgung.jpg)

#### A carefully researched formula balancing the ingredients and ratios in each portion so it can be used over a long period without any adverse effects on health (those with underlying conditions should consult a doctor).

#### Pure honey, fully ripened straight from the hive, without undergoing any heat-processing that would strip away its nutrients; "gung se" ginger — rich in nutrients, harvested around Tet; and paper lemon — a lemon variety known for being especially nutrient-rich.

#### _Processing method_: Mother Nature chose the method of drying and infusing in pure honey, because drying reduces water content, allowing the product to preserve itself naturally over a long period. The amount of lemon added is just enough to avoid raising the product's acidity, which could cause spoilage.

#### Ginger is chosen not overly ground so the product isn't too spicy, making it suitable even for children. Users can also actively chew and suck on the ginger piece when needed.

#### No extraction methods are used to isolate individual compounds, because in reality it's not possible to extract every beneficial compound from food — doing so would preserve one compound while losing others.

#### An infusion time of 4-6 months allows the ingredients to blend evenly, letting the flavors of ginger, lemon, and honey combine harmoniously.

#### 

**Recipe for making your own honey lemon ginger drink at home**:

#### 1 cup of hot warm water (180ml – 200ml), 2 teaspoons of honey, 3 slices of ginger, cover and let sit for about 2 minutes. Then squeeze in a piece of lemon. (If coughing a lot, it's best to suck on and chew both the ginger and lemon. The important thing is to keep the mixture in your throat for as long as possible.)

#### Drink twice a day, once in the morning before breakfast, and once in the afternoon.

#### 

#### ***Note**: Don't drink before sleep, as ginger can cause insomnia in some people.

#### Healthy individuals should drink 1 cup/day.
