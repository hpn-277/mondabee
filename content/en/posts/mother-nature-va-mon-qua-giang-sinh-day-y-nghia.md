Gifts are exchanged, and stores are decorated splendidly to welcome cherished guests. Mother Nature is no exception, decorating and welcoming guests at its main showroom. ## The Meaning of Christmas in Vietnam

Christmas did not originate in Vietnam but arrived along with Catholicism and Protestantism when they entered Vietnam in 1533. On Christmas Day, churches are decorated splendidly to celebrate this major holiday, which has unintentionally become a trend and a popular spot for people to check in and take photos. From there, Christmas became widely known throughout our beloved Vietnam. During the Christmas season, young couples in love also head out to meet up, dine together, and join in the bustling atmosphere of the day. Families also decorate Christmas trees with many colors, exchange gifts, and enjoy a small meal together to share stories from recent days. Although our beloved Vietnam celebrates Lunar New Year (Tet), during the final days of the solar calendar year, we too wish to gather and share time with our loved ones.
