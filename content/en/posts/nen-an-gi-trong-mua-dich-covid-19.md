**Eating nutrient-rich foods and drinking enough water every day can provide the body with essential nutrients and keep it hydrated, helping to prevent infection caused by COVID-19 as well as speeding up the treatment and recovery process.**

Here are a few dishes, foods, and drinks that are tasty, nutritious, and affordable, and can be made at home to help treat and prevent illness effectively:

**Chicken Congee with Scallion and Perilla**  
_Benefits:_ May help reduce inflammation and speed up the body's recovery; reduce nasal congestion and ease upper respiratory symptoms; it is nutritious, easy to digest, easy to cook, and helps replenish fluids and nutrients for the body.

_Ingredients (for 4 servings)_ :

+ 100g plain rice;

+ 30g glutinous rice;

+ 20g mung beans

+ 1/2 chicken

+ Dried shallots, scallions, perilla leaves, ginger

+ Other seasonings

_How to cook:_ Wash the rice and beans thoroughly; wash the scallions and perilla and chop finely; toast the dried shallots until fragrant, peel the ginger and slice thinly. Clean the chicken, boil until cooked, then shred it finely.

Add the rice and beans to the pot of chicken broth and simmer gently with the chicken bones and toasted shallots. Once the rice is fully cooked and thick, remove the bones. Add the shredded chicken meat, bring to a slightly higher boil, season to taste, sprinkle in the chopped scallions and perilla, stir well, then turn off the heat. Ladle into a bowl, sprinkle with pepper and a few thin slices of ginger, and eat while hot.

[**Ginger, Lemon, Lemongrass, and Honey Water**](https://monabee.vn/san-pham/mat-ong-nguyen-chat-ngam-chanh-gung-hu-500ml/)

_Benefits:_ Helps cleanse the upper respiratory tract; prevents respiratory infections and the Corona virus.

_Ingredients:_ Vietnamese lime: 5 pieces. Wash and peel the zest; lemongrass: 15 stalks. Wash and crush; fresh ginger: 1 root. Wash, leave the skin on, and slice thinly; honey: 2 tablespoons (20ml).

_How to make:_ Boil water, add the crushed lemongrass and stir, then add the lime zest and sliced fresh ginger and stir. Simmer gently for about 2-3 minutes, turn off the heat, and cover. Pour into a jar or glass bottle, add 2 tablespoons of honey, and stir well.

_How to use:_ Drink warm. Use one 100ml cup at a time (upon waking in the morning, midday, and before bed).

**Fish Dishes**

_Benefits:_ A source of protein that is rich in nutrients, helps reduce blood cholesterol levels, and is easy to digest; it is also a food group that helps boost the immune system; the vitamin A and omega-3 in fish help strengthen the body's immune system, improve cardiovascular health, reduce inflammation, and increase the activity of white blood cells and macrophages, protecting the body from viruses and bacteria. It also helps speed up the body's metabolism and reduce the risk of gastrointestinal cancers.

_Recommendation:_ Eat at least 200g of fish per week.

**Foods to Increase**

  * Foods rich in vitamin A and carotene (ripe yellow and orange fruits, papaya, liver): Vitamin A is essential for maintaining the integrity of the respiratory and digestive mucosa, helping form a barrier against the invasion of viruses and bacteria.
  * Foods rich in vitamin C (oranges, grapefruit, green leafy vegetables): Vitamin C helps boost immunity and reduce inflammation symptoms.
  * Foods rich in vitamin D (fish liver, eggs, or vitamin D-fortified cereals): plays a role in activating the body's immune system.
  * Nuts rich in vitamin E (almonds, sunflower seeds, pumpkin seeds, etc.): Vitamin E is a powerful antioxidant that reduces respiratory infections and supports the activity of immune cells and antibodies.
  * Foods rich in zinc (meat, seafood): Zinc is essential for the activity of immune cells, including white blood cells.
  * Foods rich in selenium (shrimp, mushrooms, eggs, beans): Selenium helps boost immunity and prevent aging.
  * Spices with antibacterial properties (garlic, ginger, onion, etc.): Their phytochemicals help fight inflammation and boost the body's immunity.
  * Yogurt: Helps increase the production of gamma interferon, which helps inhibit viral replication.
