Scientists have discovered that royal jelly contains substance R (Royal Jelly), and so far this compound has only been found to have anti-aging effects and to promote metabolism—its other effects remain scientifically undetermined (a mystery compound).

Royal jelly contains hydroxydecanoic acid, a substance that helps balance the skin's acidic environment, bringing the skin's pH back to a stable state.

Royal jelly is rich in amino acids and vitamins, so it helps boost liver function, increase detoxification in the liver, and give the skin more elasticity and smoothness.

Using royal jelly both by drinking it and applying it topically helps prolong youthfulness and maintain smooth, fresh-looking skin. ## **How to apply a royal jelly face mask**

Royal jelly is already great on its own! Just applying a **[pure fresh royal jelly mask](https://monabee.vn/2019/12/05/cach-lam-mat-na-sua-ong-chua/)** will already give your skin natural smoothness and rosy brightness. However, combining it with other natural beauty ingredients like green tea, turmeric powder, and honey will boost its effectiveness even further, with results that will surprise you.

### **Royal jelly and turmeric powder mask**

Turmeric powder contains curcumin, an active compound with antioxidant effects that fades dark spots, treats acne, and helps skin achieve a natural rosy glow.

Ingredients: 1/2 teaspoon of pure fresh royal jelly, 1/2 teaspoon of turmeric powder, 1 teaspoon of honey.

How to make it: Mix well until the mixture is combined.

How to apply: Wash your face with facial cleanser, then rinse with warm water to open your pores. Apply the mask mixture evenly to your face, **massaging continuously for 10 minutes** (this helps your face absorb the nutrients). Then rinse with warm water, followed by cold water, to tighten your pores.

Do this regularly 2 times a week to see the change.

### **Royal jelly and [Vitamin E](https://www.vinmec.com/vi/tin-tuc/thong-tin-suc-khoe/cong-dung-cua-vitamin-e/) mask**

Vitamin E can regenerate cells and limit melanin production (the factor causing dark spots and melasma); it also has antioxidant effects, retains moisture, and boosts the skin's resistance. A **royal jelly and vitamin E mask** gives you skin bursting with vitality. This mask is suitable for women with dry skin.

Ingredients: 1/2 teaspoon of royal jelly, 1 [**Vitamin E**](https://www.vinmec.com/vi/tin-tuc/thong-tin-suc-khoe/cong-dung-cua-vitamin-e/) capsule

How to make it: Extract the liquid from the vitamin E capsule and mix it with royal jelly.

How to apply: Follow the same steps as instructed above.

Do this regularly 2 times a week to see the change.

### **Royal jelly and egg yolk mask**

Egg yolk supplies moisture to the skin, helping it become softer. A royal jelly and egg yolk mask is suitable for women with dry, flaky skin that needs water to restore its moisture.

Ingredients needed: 1/2 teaspoon of royal jelly, 1/2 egg yolk, 1 teaspoon of honey, 2 drops of lemon juice.

How to apply: Mix all the prepared ingredients together in a clean bowl until you get a uniform mixture.

How to use: Use a brush to apply the mixture to your face, then massage for about 5 to 10 minutes so the nutrients absorb into your skin. Keep the mask on for another 15 minutes, then rinse with warm water, followed by cold water, to tighten your pores.

### **Royal jelly and green tea mask**

According to scientific research, green tea contains many natural nutrients such as vitamins A, C, and E, which have antioxidant, skin-beautifying, and acne-fighting effects. A royal jelly and green tea mask is suitable for skin with acne issues.

Ingredients needed: 1/2 teaspoon of royal jelly, 1 teaspoon of green tea powder, enough water.

How to make it: Mix the ingredients evenly until smooth and fine.

How to use: Apply the mixture to your face for about 15 to 30 minutes, resting and relaxing while it's on.

### **Royal jelly and oatmeal mask**

Oatmeal contains high levels of vitamins B, B6, and E, which have exfoliating effects, clean out oil and dirt, and can also fight inflammation, redness, and speed up scar healing. This mask is suitable for those with oily skin.

Ingredients needed: 1/2 teaspoon of royal jelly, 1 teaspoon of oatmeal powder, 2 teaspoons of boiling water.

How to make it: Pour boiling water over the oatmeal powder and stir until smooth, then add the royal jelly and whisk until the mixture is combined.

How to apply: Apply the royal jelly and oatmeal mask to your face for about 15 to 20 minutes. Rest and relax while the mask is applied.

### **Royal jelly and yogurt mask**

Yogurt is fermented from beneficial bacteria and carries a treasure trove of nutrients good for the skin, such as vitamins C and D and amino acids. Yogurt helps supply water, minerals, and vitamins to the skin, keeping it full of vitality. This mask is suitable for those with thin, sensitive skin.

Ingredients: 1/2 teaspoon of royal jelly, 1/3 container of unsweetened yogurt.

How to make it: Mix the ingredients together evenly.

How to apply: Apply the mixture evenly to your face, then gently massage, relax and rest for about 20 minutes, and wash your face with warm water followed by cold water, or use an ice compress to tighten your pores.

Royal jelly and honey are two "treasures" of beekeeping, considered a "miracle remedy" for women, used to nourish, exfoliate, and treat acne.

Ingredients: 1/2 spoon of royal jelly, 1 spoon of pure honey.

How to make it: Mix the ingredients evenly.

## **What you need to know about royal jelly face masks**

**How long should you apply a royal jelly mask**, does it cause itching—these are questions **Mother Nature Honey** often gets from customers. Let's explore them below!

### **How long should you apply a royal jelly mask?**

The best time to apply a royal jelly mask is about 15 to 30 minutes. Before applying the mask, you need to do the following steps to clean your face.

Step 1: Wash your hands.

Step 2: Remove makeup

Step 2: Wash your face with facial cleanser

Step 3: Exfoliate with honey once a week

Step 4: Steam your face once a week (with lemongrass, lemon, ginger, perilla leaves, etc.)

Step 5: Rinse your face with warm water

Step 6: Apply the royal jelly mask, massaging continuously for about 10 minutes, then wash your face clean.

Step 7: Then, wash your face with warm water followed by cool water to tighten your pores.

### **Can you leave a royal jelly mask on overnight?**

Because royal jelly contains many nutrients essential for the skin, many customers intend to leave a royal jelly mask on overnight so the nutrients can absorb more. However, you should know that royal jelly needs to be refrigerated—leaving it out in natural conditions for too long will reduce its quality.

Leaving a royal jelly mask on overnight creates a favorable environment for bacteria to grow and attracts insects. A royal jelly mask should not be left on overnight.

### **Does applying a royal jelly mask cause itching?**

The first time you use royal jelly as a mask, your skin may not have adjusted yet and you could experience some itching; after a few applications your skin will get used to it. To reduce itching, use an ice compress to soothe your skin.

Note that the first time you apply it, use half the amount of royal jelly called for in the recipe, and you can add water to thin the mixture so your skin can gradually adjust.

### **Should you apply a royal jelly mask every day?**

Although royal jelly is very good for the skin, every product has two sides. So you should never overuse it. You should only apply a royal jelly mask 2 times a week.
