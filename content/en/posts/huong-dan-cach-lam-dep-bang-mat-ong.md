**Using honey and turmeric for facial skincare**

Facial skin is an extremely sensitive area — signs of aging and fading beauty always show up here first. The first tip in **this guide to beauty tips using honey at home** is caring for facial skin with honey and turmeric. This combination of honey and turmeric helps sweep away this worry, with its anti-aging and nourishing properties for facial skin. Using it regularly and consistently helps break down melanin pigmentation, saying goodbye to dark spots and freckles on the face.

Instructions:

· Prepare pure honey and turmeric powder in a 2:1 ratio

· Remove makeup and thoroughly clean the face

· Mix the prepared honey and turmeric together

· Apply the mixture to the face and leave it on for about 7 minutes

· Then rinse the skin clean, and don't forget to apply a toner afterward to rebalance the skin's pH

**Using honey and egg for beauty**

Dark, sun-tanned skin and enlarged pores make many women feel self-conscious. Not to mention other types of acne on the face — but don't worry, using honey and egg will sweep all of this away from your skin, restoring its natural, rosy beauty. Here's how:

· Prepare 1 spoon of honey and 1 egg yolk

· Mix until the blend is smooth and consistent

· Exfoliate and do a basic skin cleanse

· Soak a sheet mask in the mixture, then apply the mask to your face

· Wait 5 to 10 minutes, then remove and wash your face clean with facial cleanser

· Should be done 2-3 times a week

**Full-body beauty treatment combining coffee grounds and honey**

Combining honey with coffee grounds will help strip away all the dirt on your skin's surface — say goodbye to acne-prone, dull, dark skin. Here's a simple way to do it:

· Prepare 3 coffee spoons of honey for every 100 grams of coffee grounds.

· Mix the blend thoroughly

· Wet your whole body's skin

· Apply the mixture evenly to the areas you want to treat

· Gently massage those areas to remove dirt and dead skin cells

· Rinse off with water afterward — using body wash as well is recommended for a complete clean

**Using fresh milk and honey for a whole-body whitening bath**

A blend of fresh milk and honey is gentle on the skin, yet delivers a wonderful whitening effect — did you know that already, ladies?

· Prepare 0.5 liters of fresh milk (unsweetened is recommended) and 2 spoons of honey

· Whisk the milk and honey mixture together thoroughly

· Exfoliate and cleanse the skin first. Apply the mixture evenly over the body

· Massage the skin evenly for 10 to 15 minutes

· Rinse off gently with water afterward
