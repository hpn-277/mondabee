Thanks to the gifts of nature, Ba Ria-Vung Tau is a city with many industrial crops such as rubber, coffee, and longan, which is why beekeeping has been able to develop so strongly in recent years. With honey quality highly regarded, Kim Truc Plus and the relevant authorities are gradually building a "Made in BR-VT" honey brand.

## Hanh Phuc Organic Honey

Although it has only been on the market inside and outside the province for about 4 years, the Hanh Phuc honey brand of Hanh Phuc Organic Production and Trading Services Co., Ltd., in Phu Thien hamlet, Hoa Hiep commune, Xuyen Moc district, has already won plenty of support and trust from customers in many places.

According to Ms. Do Thi My Hanh, the company's director, her family has a tradition of beekeeping spanning more than 20 years of experience. Thanks to this seniority in the industry, along with more than 5,000 beehives, they harvest nearly 500 tons of honey each year. On top of that, the company also purchases 1,000 tons annually from family-run farms to export raw honey to the US and Europe, combining nectar from various flowers such as coffee, melaleuca, longan, lychee, rubber leaf, and cashew leaf.

To build the Hanh Phuc honey brand as successfully as it has, Ms. Do Thi My Hanh has always maintained that honey is a health-care product, so it must be genuinely high quality by the time it reaches customers. That's why her bees are raised through a fully natural process. The bees are kept in coffee flower gardens and moved with the flowering seasons to gather nectar. As a result, the honey harvested always has a golden, thick, sweet consistency with a distinctive fragrance compared to honey raised through industrial methods. Beyond exporting raw honey abroad, the company is investing in researching and developing honey-based products such as royal jelly capsules and turmeric starch with royal jelly, to meet the increasingly high demands of consumers.

Ms. Hanh shared with reporters, "To bring good products to consumers, we focus on investing in product quality. Our honey is raised and processed according to Organic standards. Thanks to this good product quality, our Hanh Phuc honey has quickly been embraced by consumers. To date, products under the Hanh Phuc honey brand have been distributed in many places inside and outside the province, with an annual consumption of nearly 20 tons of honey and 100kg of fresh royal jelly."

## Building an Image of Natural Honey That's Close to Customers

With the Mother Nature honey brand, Kim Truc Plus Co., Ltd., in Dong Hai hamlet, Tan Hai commune, Phu My town, is also constantly striving to build a "Made in Ba Ria - Vung Tau" honey product. Not only investing in raw materials by owning a beekeeping area integrated with nature, Mother Nature honey is now also focused on combining honey with various herbs to produce health-beneficial honey products such as lemon ginger honey, turmeric-steeped honey, Ngoc Linh ginseng honey, and royal jelly. With such a diverse product range, Mother Nature brings around 10,000 products to market each year.

Ms. Nguyen Thanh Truc, Deputy Director of Kim Truc Plus Co., Ltd., said the raw materials the company produces are made entirely from nature, and the company holds safety certifications for its products, licensed by specialized authorities. With the desire to expand beyond the domestic market, the company is also working to improve not only quality but also product design, aiming to export its products abroad to countries such as Canada, the United States, and across Asia.

"We always strive to bring the market the best honey products with the highest nutritional value, combined with natural herbs to properly care for consumers' health," Ms. Truc said.

## Building Honey Products with Local Character

Beekeeping households in the province are currently concentrated mostly in two districts, Chau Duc and Xuyen Moc, with nearly 150 households in total. According to the agricultural sector's assessment, these two localities have many advantages for developing beekeeping, thanks to an abundance of melaleuca, rubber, and other industrial crops such as cashew and coffee - beekeeping here produces delicious, high-quality honey with a strikingly glossy color.

With the goal of building a distinctive local product (OCOP), beekeeping households in the province are paying increasing attention to factors that ensure product quality, developing honey into a sustainable, distinctive local product for the future, and focusing on building the brand methodically. With support from local authorities, these units are currently preparing OCOP product dossiers, rushing to complete steps such as trademark registration and product traceability labeling.

To date, several products such as pure honey, longan flower honey, coffee flower honey, and rambutan flower honey from Duy Anh Bee Co., Ltd. have been recognized as achieving 4-star OCOP product status in 2020.

Mr. Trinh Duc Toan, Deputy Head of the Sub-Department of Agricultural, Forestry, and Fishery Product Quality Management (under the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development), said: Every year, the sub-department takes honey samples from beekeeping facilities to test for food safety criteria. These criteria all meet the required standards.

"For honey products to be exported abroad, several issues need attention: product quality must meet international standards; the volume of honey must also be stable enough to meet orders when exporting overseas. Businesses must work closely together with beekeepers to build a long-term supply of raw materials to meet the volumes needed for export," Mr. Toan added.
