Not merely a place to showcase products, Monabee's booth was also a meeting point for exchange and connection, where stories about_** _the journey of beekeeping, honey harvesting, and preserving the purity of nature_** _were shared with visitors from Vietnam and abroad._

## 2. The Appeal of the Monabee Booth - When Nature Touches the Heart of Those Who Experience It

In the early days of August, as Hanoi went through the peak heat of summer, the atmosphere at the **Thang Long Imperial Citadel** became livelier than ever with the occurrence of **Vietnam OCOPEX 2025** - the OCOP Export Fair, part of a series of activities celebrating **National Day (September 2)**.

Despite the sweltering weather, the Monabee team's spirit remained on fire. Everyone shared the same wish: to bring pure, high-quality honey products closer to people everywhere, and further still, to international friends.

![Monabee attracts international customers](/images/posts/monabee-tai-vietnam-ocopex-2025-ket-noi-giao/a.jpg)_**Monabee attracts international customers**_

Throughout the event, the Monabee booth welcomed a great many visitors. Not only domestic consumers but also many international guests expressed particular interest in the herbal honey products.

What made Monabee leave such a strong impression was the sweet, pure flavor of naturally ripened honey - honey harvested at exactly the right time to ensure it retains all its nutrients, vitamins, and beneficial enzymes.

In addition, Monabee's product collection is also highly diverse:

  * **Pure honey** – golden as sunlight, rich in nutrients.
  * **Lemon ginger honey** – lightly sour, mildly spicy, good for overall health.
  * **Kumquat mint honey** – cooling and fresh, helps reduce coughs, good for the respiratory system.
  * **Turmeric royal jelly honey** – combines beauty care with health care from within.



All are made using modern technology, packed in glass jars with refined packaging, and meet the **OCOP 4-star** standard. Customers were not only able to sample the products directly, but also to listen to stories about the production process, the origin of the ingredients, and real-life stories from the beekeepers themselves.

Many visitors did not hesitate to buy on the spot to bring home as gifts - not just gifts of health, but gifts steeped in the flavor of Vietnam.

## Vietnam Ocopex 2025 - A Bridge Bringing Monabee to International Markets

Vietnam OCOPEX 2025 is not just a trade fair, but also a forum for trade connections, where OCOP businesses have the opportunity to reach distributors, importers, and potential partners both at home and abroad.

![Vietnam Ocopex 2025 - A bridge bringing Monabee to international markets](/images/posts/monabee-tai-vietnam-ocopex-2025-ket-noi-giao/bbb.jpg)_**Vietnam Ocopex 2025 - A bridge bringing Monabee to international markets**_

 

As the sole representative of Ba Ria - Vung Tau at the event, Monabee took advantage of this opportunity to:

  * Introduce the strengths of local agricultural products to the international community.
  * Learn about the tastes, needs, and import standards of each market.
  * Strengthen cooperation with distributors and expand its sales network.



Quite a few partners expressed a desire for long-term cooperation, to help Monabee honey grow strongly into new markets. This is a positive signal, opening the door to broader distribution for this authentically Vietnamese honey product.

## The Pride of Monabee Honey, the Essence of Ba Ria - Vung Tau's Nature

Every drop of Monabee honey is a crystallization of heaven and earth, of the skillful hands of beekeepers, and of a deep respect for nature. Thanks to the distinctive climate and soil of Ba Ria - Vung Tau, the bee colonies are raised naturally, without chemicals, yielding a golden, fragrant, and nutrient-rich honey.

Monabee not only preserves traditional beekeeping methods but also applies science and technology to control quality, ensure food safety and hygiene, and preserve the full nutritional value of the honey.

The mission Monabee pursues is to:

  * **Spread the value of clean agricultural products - to OCOP standard.**
  * **Affirm the identity of the Vietnamese brand** on the world map of agriculture.
  * **Bring the essence of Vietnamese honey further afield** , becoming a source of pride for the Vietnamese people.



## The Event Concludes - The Beginning of a New Journey

Vietnam OCOPEX 2025 closed with many beautiful memories and proud achievements. The Monabee booth not only left an impression in the hearts of visitors, but also opened up many opportunities for cooperation and sustainable development in the future.

This event once again affirmed that:

  * **Trade connection** is not just an opportunity to sell, but also a bridge of culture and friendship.
  * **Market access** is a strategic step to expand scale and affirm product quality.
  * **Affirming position** is proof of the brand's constant effort on its journey to conquer markets at home and abroad.



Monabee will continue to improve without stopping, maintaining its commitment to **100% naturally ripened honey** , contributing to the development of clean agriculture in Vietnam and elevating the value of Vietnamese agricultural products in the international market.
