Honey has been proven to be a food rich in various nutrients such as enzymes, amino acids, and minerals. With its light sweetness—sweeter than ordinary sugar—honey needs to be used skillfully to properly supply nutrients to children. Today's article from Mother Nature will guide you on ways to **_prepare honey for children_** so it's delicious and appealing, with kids loving it and not skipping meals.

## Preparing honey for children by combining it with oats

[_Oats_](https://tintuc.vn/yen-mach-la-gi-cong-dung-ky-dieu-cua-yen-mach-post1234106?utm_source=tkdpc&utm_medium=ads2&gclid=CjwKCAiA_omPBhBBEiwAcg7sma5kw1_BFA_OB-rpeI4zLGmPKfm3w-yU31n7MEaQdUKjRe32Ml2FAxoCNl0QAvD_BwE) are a grain rich in protein and soluble fiber, and are also voted one of the healthiest grains on our planet. Feeding children oats is very good for their development. To **_prepare honey for children_** by combining it with oats, you can do the following:

  * Mix honey into oatmeal and stir the mixture well
  * Spread a layer of honey on toast
  * Add honey to yogurt and mix until combined



Please note that when feeding children honey, they need to be older than 12 months—younger children may experience negative health effects.

## Preparing honey for children to eat with baked goods

Children love eating baked goods, so why not combine honey into these treats? Simply replace ordinary sugar with honey when baking. Typically, 1 cup of sugar can be replaced with half a cup of honey, adjusted to your taste. But remember that honey has a stronger sweetness than sugar, so you need to reduce the amount to suit your taste. A little tip for parents: when cooking, add ¼ teaspoon of salt for every cup of honey—this will significantly reduce the sourness in what you're cooking.

## Important notes when preparing honey for children

Honey isn't hard to prepare, but to **_prepare honey for children_** effectively while preserving as many nutrients as possible, parents should also keep the following points in mind:

  * It's recommended to use honey with warm water rather than hot water so as not to lose nutrients, and also because children can absorb it more easily than drinking honey directly.
  * Children should use it about 1 hour before eating so it doesn't affect their meals, helping them enjoy their main meal more.
  * Honey products soaked with lemon and ginger can help treat respiratory illnesses in children.
  * If a child has a stomachache or diarrhea, stop giving them honey immediately.



Keep these notes in mind so you can give honey to your children the right way, parents!

## Notes on choosing honey to prepare for children

Parents should note that although honey is very good, there are also some requirements that must be followed before giving it to children:

  * Children over 1 year old can use honey, but only in a limited (small) amount
  * Children over 10 years old can use honey more freely, at a dosage similar to adults



Using honey seems simple, but there are actually quite a few points to keep in mind, right?
