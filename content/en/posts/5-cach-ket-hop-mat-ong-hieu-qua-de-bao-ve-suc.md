## Fermented honey

The fourth way to combine honey, which you can also try at home, is fermented honey. Honey used directly is already great, but fermented honey is even better for the body. Since ancient times, our ancestors have shown the effectiveness of fermented honey combined with various natural herbs to enhance its medicinal properties. Fermented honey basically retains all the benefits of pure honey, but combining it with fermenting ingredients boosts its effectiveness significantly.
