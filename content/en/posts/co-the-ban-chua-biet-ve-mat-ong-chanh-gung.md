?DID YOU KNOW

– **LEMON** is rich in vitamin C, prevents cancer, heart disease, and aging, and supports weight loss by burning more fat. Lemon also helps balance the body's pH, reduces issues related to indigestion, bloating, constipation, acts as a diuretic, reduces the risk of high blood pressure and varicose veins, and reduces fever and chills.

In addition, **Lemon** is a fruit-bearing plant belonging to the citrus genus. When ripe, the lemon fruit turns yellow, while limes stay green. The two common types today—yellow lemons and green limes—are both edible. Lemon is mainly used as food and in beverages.

The structure of a lemon consists of two parts: the outer peel and the inner flesh with its segments. Lemon juice is up to 80% water and 5% (about 0.3 mol/liter) citric acid—the substance that gives lemon its sour taste. Lemon's pH is 2–3, making it acidic. Besides its role as a culinary seasoning, lemon has a wide range of uses, including beauty, health care, [household cleaning](https://www.cleanipedia.com/vn/ve-sinh-phong-tam/cach-tang-cuong-ve-sinh-nha-cua-vao-thoi-diem-giao-mua.html), and disinfecting.
