> You may not know it, but **ginger** is a familiar spice in the daily life of every family. But beyond its use as food, [****ginger's benefits****](https://benh.vn/cong-dung-ky-dieu-cua-cu-gung-2557/) for human health are also very positive and beneficial. In this article, let's find out what benefits ginger has to offer!

 

![](/images/posts/cong-dung-tuyet-voi-cua-gung-doi-voi-suc-khoe/gung.png)

 

### ****Treats colds****

One of the useful benefits of ginger that we can't overlook is treating colds and fevers caused by unpredictable weather changes. Ginger has a spicy, pungent taste and a warming nature; when used with warm water, it dilates capillaries, speeds up the sweating process, supports blood circulation, and warms the body from the inside out to fight respiratory syncytial virus—the cause of several respiratory illnesses like colds, fever, and coughs. In addition, people often cook fresh ginger with alcohol for "coin scraping" (cạo gió) to relieve cold symptoms; ginger and ginger essential oil are also good for clearing phlegm and are very effective for various respiratory issues such as flu, coughs, asthma, bronchitis, and difficulty breathing.

### ****Anti-inflammatory****

Ginger also has effective anti-inflammatory properties without causing stomach ulcers like other anti-inflammatory medications. Because ginger has such strong anti-inflammatory ability, many arthritis medications today contain ingredients extracted from ginger. One study found that most users who used ginger to treat inflammation and rheumatism saw a marked reduction in pain and swelling.

In addition, ginger's anti-inflammatory properties are very useful for treating gum and periodontal inflammation and preventing oral health issues like canker sores, tooth decay, and dental abscesses. You can regularly drink hot ginger tea or rinse your mouth with ginger water to protect your oral cavity and reduce the risk of dental disease.

### ****Fights cancer****

Ginger contains many powerful antioxidants that have anti-inflammatory effects and can prevent the activity of certain types of cancer. Some studies have shown that the compounds in ginger can reduce the risk of ovarian cancer and colorectal cancer, and protect skin from UV rays—a cause of skin cancer.

![](/images/posts/cong-dung-tuyet-voi-cua-gung-doi-voi-suc-khoe/gung1.jpg)

 

### ****Reduces stress and headaches****

Another benefit of ginger that can't be overlooked is its ability to reduce headaches, relieve mental fatigue, and eliminate states of tension such as dizziness, restlessness, unease, and anxiety. When your body is tired, tense, or has a headache, simply chewing a piece of fresh ginger for about 30 minutes will noticeably reduce these symptoms.

Additionally, ginger helps prolong the life of brain cells and provides antioxidants that prevent harmful oxidation to brain cells. Therefore, ginger helps fight symptoms of memory decline (due to age), especially reducing the risk of Alzheimer's disease.

### ****Supports relief for back and shoulder pain****

Many people who don't move around much and sit in one position for long periods often experience symptoms like back pain and shoulder/neck pain. When experiencing back and shoulder pain, you should use hot ginger water with a bit of salt and vinegar, or use a towel soaked with a mixture of fresh ginger and honey and apply it to the painful area several times. This method helps relax the muscles, improves blood circulation, and effectively reduces pain.

 

### ****Supports weight loss****

Obesity and weight gain, which worry many people, often stem from a disrupted metabolism. Ginger is also believed to help maintain your figure by keeping your metabolism stable, which is important since intensive dieting often slows it down. Once your metabolism slows down, the weight you've lost will quickly come back once you stop dieting. A daily dose of ginger can help prevent other related issues.
