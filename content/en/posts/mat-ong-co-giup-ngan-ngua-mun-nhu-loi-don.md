## Using honey and turmeric to treat acne

The 2 main ingredients, honey and turmeric, are both easy to find. Besides slowing skin aging, turmeric also has anti-inflammatory, antiseptic properties, and can even reduce redness and swelling on the skin's surface. How to use turmeric **_and honey to prevent acne_**:

  * Prepare a tablespoon of honey and 1/8 teaspoon of turmeric powder
  * Mix until the blend feels even
  * Apply to the acne-affected skin area and leave on for 20 minutes
  * Rinse your face clean with warm water, and don't forget to pat it dry with a towel
  * Apply moisturizer to your face

This should be done regularly until acne clears up and helps prevent it from coming back.

## Honey and apple, a mixture that fights off hidden pimples

Apple combined with **_honey to prevent acne_** works very well, because apples contain plenty of vitamins that, when mixed with honey, create a mixture with strong anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and antioxidant properties. That's why this mixture is an effective method for eliminating hidden pimples, bringing positive results for your skin.

How to use the honey and apple mixture to prevent hidden pimples is also extremely simple:

  * Peel an apple and blend it into a puree using a blender. Then mix it with a suitable amount of honey until the mixture is uniform
  * Wash your face clean
  * Apply the mixture to areas with hidden pimples and wait 15 minutes
  * Rinse your face clean with warm water afterward

## Using honey and lemon to tackle blackheads

Feeling frustrated by blackheads? Don't worry — the honey and lemon combo is here. It'll clear up all the blackheads on your face right away.

Ingredients to prepare:

  * 2 to 3 fresh lemons, 5 to 6 spoons of honey
  * Squeeze the lemon juice and mix it with the honey
  * Stir until the mixture becomes uniform
  * Apply the mixture to the affected skin area and wait 30 minutes
  * Gently massage the area, then rinse clean with warm water afterward

Using this method regularly will noticeably improve your skin. However, keep in mind you should avoid direct sun exposure afterward, since the skin will be extremely sensitive at that point. Regular use not only tackles blackheads but also helps prevent them from appearing, giving you the smooth, dreamy skin you've always wanted.
