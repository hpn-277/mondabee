Tet is gradually arriving across our country, and in Vietnamese custom and tradition, candies and fruit jams are indispensable. Besides the familiar jams like coconut jam and candied ginger, have you ladies ever tried **_making honey candy_** or other types of jam with honey? If not, let's join Mother Nature in learning how to make a few types of candy and jam using the familiar ingredient — honey.

## Guide to making chewy honey orange jam

To **_make this chewy honey_** orange jam, first you need to prepare the following ingredients:

  * Yellow oranges
  * Sugar
  * Passion fruit juice
  * Honey

After washing the oranges, soak them in boiling water for 2 to 3 minutes, then remove and soak them in ice-cold water for 1 minute. Slice the oranges into small, bite-sized pieces. Then add the passion fruit juice, sugar, and honey mixture into a pan and simmer over low heat. Keep simmering the mixture until the sugar has fully dissolved, then add the orange slices in. When the orange and sugar mixture starts to thicken, turn off the heat. Take the orange pieces and dry them under the sun until dry, then store them in a glass jar — they're ready to eat.

## Guide to making honey lollipops

Making handmade honey candy isn't as difficult as many people think. Let Mother Nature reveal the recipe to you ladies. The essential ingredients for this treat include:

  * Sugar
  * Honey
  * Water
  * Lemon juice

Making these lollipops is also fairly simple — just follow the steps in order:

  * Stir the water, sugar, and honey mixture evenly in a pot over very low heat, letting the sugar dissolve gradually and completely
  * Raise the temperature to 150°C so the sugar solidifies — you won't need to stir much at this point. This step ends when the sugar turns a clear, sparkling golden color.
  * Pour the mixture into a glass jar with a pouring spout, add the lemon juice, and stir evenly to blend it in
  * Pour the candy onto parchment paper or into molds to create attractive shapes

Wishing you success making your own batches of honey candy.

## Lemon gummy candy: seems hard, but it's actually easy

Making **_honey candy_** to treat guests during Tet — have you heard of lemon gummy candy? This is a treat that both children and adults love. The ingredients you need include: sugar, water, 2 lemons, gelatin powder, and honey.

  * Put the sugar and water on the stove to simmer
  * Mix the gelatin powder with hot water, then pour it into the simmering sugar water. Simmer for 20 minutes, then add the lemon juice and simmer together for another 5 minutes.
  * Add honey into the simmering lemon-sugar water, stir gently and mix thoroughly.
  * Prepare a tray and aluminum foil, brushed with a thin layer of oil, before pouring in the candy mixture.
  * Add finely chopped lemon peel. Place it in the refrigerator to let the candy set.
  * The final step is cutting the candy into bite-sized pieces, then coating them with powdered sugar or white sugar crystals for extra appeal and visual interest.

Lay it out on a tray and invite everyone to enjoy!

## How to make delicious honey and strawberry jam

This is a fairly popular type of jam thanks to its delicious flavor and ease of use, especially for busy mornings — no fuss, no time-consuming prep, yet still a nutritious breakfast. To make this honey-infused strawberry jam, you'll need strawberries, honey, and lemon juice.

  * Cut the strawberries into small pieces, then mix them with lemon juice and honey. Place over very low heat and stir back and forth until the mixture thickens. It's best to let it soak for 2-3 hours before simmering it down into jam.
  * Heat a pan, pour in the strawberry jam mixture, keep the heat very low, and let the strawberry jam simmer down. Mash it with a spoon so the strawberries become smooth — during this step, remember to stir quickly so it doesn't burn. Turn off the heat once the mixture has thickened.
  * Let it cool, then put it into a glass jar and use it gradually.

This honey strawberry jam isn't difficult at all, is it, ladies?
