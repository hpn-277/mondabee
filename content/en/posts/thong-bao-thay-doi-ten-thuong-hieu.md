Mother Nature is pleased to announce it will be changing its brand name in the near future. This is one of the decisions made as part of the brand's development strategy.

![](/images/posts/thong-bao-thay-doi-ten-thuong-hieu/366732902_2613724055459014_5943832875271801713_n.jpg)Over the years, the name Mother Nature has left a deep impression in the hearts of our users, becoming a companion to many individuals and families on their journey of health care and protection. To bring an even more complete experience, the company has decided to give the brand a new look, changing its name from **Mother Nature** to **Monabee**.

"Mo" in Mother stands for mother

"Na" in Nature stands for nature

"Bee" stands for bee

In addition, "Mona" also appears in the name of the mysterious, beautiful Mona Lisa, from the painting of the same name by the famous artist Leonardo da Vinci

**Monabee** carries not only the founder's dedication, but is also a pure gift of health bestowed by mother nature, hoping to send a golden message about proactive health care, listening to your body, cherishing natural products, loving yourself, and pursuing a green health journey

The products are still honey-based, but in this new version, you'll notice a fresh new look in branding and packaging. Notably, for the first time, distinctive authentic Vietnamese herbs such as paper lemon, native ginger, and glutinous turmeric are freeze-dried at deep sub-zero temperatures before being steeped in naturally ripened honey, with improved preservation and usability. Moreover, recognizing the strength of its available supply of medicinal ingredients along with the fact that the domestic herbal honey market has not yet been optimally tapped, **Monabee** wants to continue writing the story of this wonderful gift, researching and processing products from pure honey to enhance their nutritional and medicinal value.

Along with this change in brand identity, **Monabee** also promises to bring more products suited to the needs of a wider range of users.

![](/images/posts/thong-bao-thay-doi-ten-thuong-hieu/93d17ad6bb2b6c75353a.jpg)Fundamentally, **Monabee** is a continuation and development of Mother Nature's core values, but with a new look and ongoing improvements in processing technology and creativity in its product range and approach to users. This is not meant to compare the quality or effectiveness between **Monabee** and Mother Nature.

Once again, thank you to our customers and partners for the trust and companionship you've given Mother Nature over the years. We hope **Monabee** will receive even more of that same wonderful affection from you.
