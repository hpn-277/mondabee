## How does ginger prevent cancer?

Recent studies have shown that **_ginger prevents cancer_** by causing cancer cells to undergo apoptosis, or programmed cell death. Scientists are now researching how to use ginger as a natural treatment for this dreadful disease. Ginger has the ability to block the spread of cancer cells to other parts of a patient's body, such as the breast, colon, rectum, liver, lungs, prostate, pancreas, and other dangerous malignant tumors.

## Compounds that help ginger prevent cancer

Inside every ginger root are many special compounds that fight the growth of cancer cells, such as gingerols, shogaols, zingerones, zerumbones, pungent oleoresin, terpenoids, and flavonoids. But the most outstanding and notable are 6-gingerol and 6-shogaol — their ability to destroy cancer cells has surprised many, since their medicinal potency is said to be 10,000 times stronger than taxol, the compound used in today's extremely costly chemotherapy treatments.

When free radicals become oxidized in the body, they create favorable conditions for cancer cells to form and grow freely, gradually giving rise to malignant tumors. That's when the anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antibacterial compounds in ginger step in to inhibit the growth of cancer cells. These active compounds stimulate the process of cancer cells self-destructing. Thanks to carrying more than 100 anti-cancer compounds, ginger helps reduce the amount of genes and proteins we absorb into our bodies — and when the primary "food" for cancer cells is reduced, their ability to multiply is reduced as well.
