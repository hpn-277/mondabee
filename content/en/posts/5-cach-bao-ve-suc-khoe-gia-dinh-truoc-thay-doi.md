No one can deny how much weather affects human health. This is most noticeable when the weather suddenly shifts from freezing cold to sweltering heat and back again. Changes in weather make us prone to symptoms like aches, fatigue, listlessness, and a lack of energy for work. Today's article from Mother Nature will help you learn how to protect the health of every family member whenever the weather changes.

## What is changing weather?

Weather is a collection of atmospheric conditions occurring at a given moment—things like sun, rain, heat, cold, and sweltering or freezing spells. Weather is strongly governed by air pressure, and the difference in surface temperatures creates pressure differences that cause weather changes.

## Symptoms that signal declining health

When weather changes occur, they bring along changes in human health. Some common signs of declining health include:

  * Changes in blood pressure—temperature shifts cause blood vessels in the body to constrict (this is a leading cause of increased blood pressure)
  * Headaches, caused by unstable blood flow to the brain
  * Sore throats, which commonly occur in sensitive individuals when the weather suddenly turns cold
  * Joint pain that flares up whenever the weather changes



And other symptoms such as seasonal flu, sneezing, and more.

## 5 ways to protect your family's health through changing weather

5 ways to protect the health of your family members during these days of unpredictable weather:

  * Exercise regularly, stay active, and get some sunlight. Physical activity helps burn excess energy and effectively reduces stress
  * Maintain a suitable diet and nutrition. Eat plenty of vegetables, yogurt products, honey, and fruits rich in vitamin C such as oranges and lemons.
  * Drink 1.5 to 2 liters of water a day to cleanse the body
  * Wear a mask to avoid viruses spread through the respiratory tract
  * Keep an optimistic, cheerful, and positive spirit



Boosting your own and your family's health and resistance is essential to staying protected amid today's constantly changing weather.
