## Applying honey to the face as a mask

The first beauty method with honey is simply applying honey evenly onto your face. This method is both simple and delivers an extremely good moisturizing effect that's no worse than any sheet mask on the market today.

Is this way of **_using honey for beauty difficult_**? No, it's extremely simple.

  * Prepare 1 spoon of honey
  * Clean your face once with water, warm water is recommended. Pat dry with a soft towel afterward so skin feels taut and clean
  * Apply the honey slowly and evenly across your entire face
  * Leave it on for 20 minutes
  * Rinse your face thoroughly clean with cold water

## Drinking warm water mixed with honey

This method will give you truly beautiful, rosy skin by helping excrete toxins from the body and cleanse harmful impurities, giving you strong internal health that significantly improves your facial skin condition.

  * Prepare a spoon of honey and a cup of warm water
  * Stir the honey into the warm water evenly until it dissolves completely
  * Drink the honey mixed with warm water in the morning right after waking up, on an empty stomach, so your body absorbs the nutrients better
  * Don't combine it with sugar, and don't use too much honey.

## Combining honey with salt for bright, radiant skin

The combination of honey and salt is simple but delivers a surprisingly effective whitening result — did you ladies know about this already? If not, take a moment to read on — this is a simple trick you can easily pick up, and the results are nothing to scoff at:

  * Prepare 2 teaspoons of sea salt and 4 teaspoons of honey
  * Mix the two ingredients thoroughly, stirring until the mixture reaches a texture thick enough to apply
  * Apply the mixture to the skin and leave it on for 10 to 15 minutes
  * Rinse thoroughly with warm water to wash away dead skin cells

Salt has an exfoliating effect, helping skin become smoother and firmer. Combined with honey, this is definitely the perfect pair to help you achieve the skin you've been dreaming of.

Honey and turmeric, an effective way to whiten skin

Turmeric is well known for its ability to prevent oxidation and slow down skin aging. Turmeric also has whitening properties and fades scars left behind by acne. A honey and turmeric mixture sweeps away dull, sallow skin.

  * Prepare honey and turmeric in a 2:1 ratio
  * Stir the mixture until it becomes uniform
  * Apply it to your face — remember to apply just a moderate layer, not too thick. Leave it on for 10 to 15 minutes.
  * Use warm water to rinse the surface completely clean.
