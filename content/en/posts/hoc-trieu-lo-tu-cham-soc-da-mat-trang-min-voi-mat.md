The "bare-faced queen" Zhao Lusi reveals that one of her golden secrets to flawlessly smooth skin is [drinking honey](https://monabee.vn/2024/07/11/hoc-trieu-lo-tu-cham-soc-da-mat-trang-min-voi-mat-ong/) every day.

## Zhao Lusi's natural honey skincare recipe

Always appearing with a radiant face, smooth beautiful skin, and captivating looks, the young star Zhao Lusi has earned the nickname "bare-faced queen." Born in 1998, she often shares her skincare tips on the Xiaohongshu platform. Most recently, Zhao Lusi revealed a daily drink recipe that helps her cool the body, detoxify, and naturally brighten her skin. What's special is that every ingredient is completely natural and easy to prepare — any woman can make it.

Zhao Lusi's recipe includes:

– Two yellow lemons.

– Two passion fruits.

– Honey

How to make it:

– Wash the yellow lemons, pat them dry with a towel, then slice thinly and remove the seeds. Arrange the lemon slices in a glass jar.

– Wash the passion fruit, scoop out the pulp, and add it to the glass jar.

– Pour in honey until it covers the lemon. The amount of honey can be adjusted to taste.

– Seal the glass jar tightly. Let it sit at room temperature for 10 days before using. Note: do not store it in the refrigerator.

Zhao Lusi mixes this blend with warm water and drinks it every morning. Lemon in general contains a high amount of vitamin C, a natural antioxidant that keeps skin firm, bright, and smooth.

Honey is a wonderful food that's not only good for health but also helps nourish beauty. With its antibacterial properties, honey helps prevent inflammatory acne and dark spots, giving us bright, healthy-looking skin.

Importantly, drinking honey with lemon in the morning is considered an effective natural detox method, helping cleanse the body from within, which in turn makes skin firmer and more radiant.

## Why is drinking honey good for skin?

Scientists have proven that honey has excellent antibacterial properties. That's why honey can be stored for a very long time if kept properly. As a result, drinking honey helps balance bacteria on the skin and clear pores. Furthermore, honey contains organic acids that help cleanse the skin and prevent acne from forming.

In addition, pure honey also stimulates the regeneration of new skin cells, fading dark spots, melasma, and freckles, and helping skin become brighter and smoother.

The vitamin E and minerals in honey help nourish skin, keeping it soft and effectively addressing dryness and dehydration. Most importantly, this golden food contains a large amount of antioxidants that help protect skin from harmful environmental factors while effectively reducing signs of aging and preventing wrinkles.
