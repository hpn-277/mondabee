_Tet is approaching, an occasion to bring people together and spread good values. Understanding this, Monabee has partnered with Le Duong Bao Lam and many other brands to bring meaningful gifts to those facing hardship this Tet 2025._

![](/images/posts/cung-monabee-va-le-duong-bao-lam-mang-tet-am-ap/3cfc1b44-8fe5-4e3a-89b5-d2dcae26d538.png)Join Le Duong Bao Lam in sending warm Tet gifts to those in need

## One spring gift – many precious hearts

The spring gifts from Monabee and other brands aren't just a culmination of dedication and love—they also carry practical value for everyday life:  
Quality regional specialties: products rich in traditional flavor, carefully selected from renowned localities.  
Practical gifts: including items that support daily life and protect health, helping to bring about a warm Tet.

## Monabee honey – a companion to your health

One of the standout products in the gift package is Monabee pure honey—a beloved natural superfood known for its wonderful health benefits:  
– Boosts resistance: drinking honey with warm water daily helps protect the respiratory system and boost resistance.  
– Supports cough relief: combining honey with herbs like ginger and lemon helps reduce cough symptoms and soothe the throat, while especially keeping the body warm during cold weather.  
Monabee hopes that these simple yet meaningful gifts will contribute a bit of joy and moral support to families facing hardship.

Wishing everyone a joyful, prosperous, and happy Tet surrounded by family!
