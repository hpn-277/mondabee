## ****2\. Drinking Honey in the Afternoon – Helps Replenish Energy****

In the afternoon, the interval between lunch and dinner, the body tends to feel most tired and depleted, and __"demands"__ a large amount of energy. At this point, the body is in a __"hungry"__ state, and a cup of warm honey water will clear the chaos in the brain, while helping the mind feel more alert and clear.

The mixed nutrients in honey are absorbed by the body very quickly, improving the nutritional condition of the blood. Drinking a cup of honey water at the right time helps replenish energy and provides a foundation to fuel activities for the next 2-3 hours. In particular, for athletes or people engaged in high-intensity physical activity, drinking a cup of honey water before and after strenuous work is also very beneficial for the body.

__How to do it: Drink a cup of honey tea or honey milk in the afternoon.__

The Chinese have a saying: Salt water each morning, honey water each night. This means: drink salt water on an empty stomach when you wake up early each day, and drink honey water each night before going to bed.

Honey, sugar, and vitamins can regulate the function of the nervous system, reduce stress and fatigue, and promote deep sleep with no side effects. A cup of honey water before bed can help you relieve stress while significantly improving your sleep quality.

In addition, the habit of drinking honey before bed also helps you lose weight, as your body begins to burn more fat at night. Pure honey contains only 64 calories per cup and will give you a feeling of fullness throughout the night.

## ****4\. Drinking Honey After a Meal – Promotes Digestion****

Honey typically helps regulate the functions of the digestive system, causing the stomach to secrete more acidic compounds than usual. In particular, after a full meal, the stomach's digestive function begins to decline, causing indigestion. A cup of honey water helps support the role of intestinal motility, promoting and improving normal stomach acid secretion, shortening bowel movement time quickly, giving you a __"lighter stomach"__ feeling. This also helps limit and reduce the negative effects that dinners can have on your stomach.

__How to do it: Drink a cup of honey water 1-2 hours after a meal.__

## ****5\. Drinking Honey Before a Meal – Prevents Stomach Acid****

At the same time, honey plays a regulatory role in normalizing the stomach's acid secretion activity. Half an hour before a meal, a cup of honey water can suppress stomach acid secretion, thereby reducing irritation to the stomach lining. For those with stomach inflammation or ulcers, it's recommended to drink a cup of warm honey water with a bit of turmeric powder (if available) each day (10ml of honey mixed with a cup of warm water and turmeric); after some time, the inflammation will noticeably reduce and heal, and may even fully recover.

__How to do it: Drink a cup of honey water about half an hour before a meal.__
