## How to Use Lemon Ginger Honey to Protect the Voice

To get the best results from the product, you need to use it properly. **_Giving the gift of lemon ginger honey_** without instructions for use just won't do.

  * Mix 20 grams of lemon ginger honey with 200ml of warm water; the temperature should be below 50 degrees Celsius to preserve the beneficial compounds in the honey
  * It's best to drink it before dinner or in the morning right after waking up
  * For treating symptoms of colds and coughs, drink more frequently (2 to 3 times a day). Sucking on and chewing the lemon and ginger pieces will significantly reduce coughing

## A Few Small Notes When Giving the Gift of Lemon Ginger Honey

Although this is an effective method for improving the voice, there are still a few things to keep in mind during use to achieve the best results:

  * Some people are more prone to honey allergies, including those with digestive function disorders, a history of low blood pressure, or diabetes
  * The product's effectiveness depends on the severity of the user's voice loss and their current health condition. Sometimes, it takes persistent use over a period of time to achieve the expected results
  * While using the product, avoid smoking and nervous system stimulants such as alcohol and beer. These substances can undermine the effects of this lemon ginger honey remedy
  * Avoid drinking it before bed if you want a good night's sleep, as the ginger content may make it harder for some people to fall asleep
  * Women before and after giving birth should use it according to a doctor's guidance
