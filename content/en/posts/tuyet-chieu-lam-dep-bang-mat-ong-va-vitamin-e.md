## Methods for Beautifying with Honey and Vitamin E

**_Beautifying with honey and vitamin E_** is very simple - here are a few methods you can try right at home.

  * Mix the liquid inside a vitamin E capsule with honey, then apply it to the skin and massage so the mixture absorbs evenly into the skin cells for the best effect.
  * Mix 2 spoons of honey, 1 vitamin E capsule, and warm water together, stir until the mixture fully dissolves, and drink as normal.



The two methods above are simple and can be used directly. While we're at it, let's also introduce a way to combine this with unsweetened yogurt to create a quality skincare mixture.

  * Take 2 to 3 vitamin E capsules and separate out the liquid
  * 2 spoons of yogurt, preferably unsweetened
  * 1 spoon of lemon juice
  * 1 spoon of pure honey



Once you've prepared the ingredients, simply mix the mixture together, clean your face thoroughly, then apply the mixture, wait for 30 minutes, and clean your skin again afterward.

Keeping this up will give you beautiful skin that will amaze even you - a bright, fair, smooth complexion is no longer out of reach. This mixture removes harmful substances from the skin, moisturizes it, and keeps it always looking youthful.
