There is no shortage of domestic honey brands in the Vietnamese market. However, precisely because it's so popular, there are also many fake and low-quality honey brands out there. These are honeys that aren't pure, blended with other solvents, which significantly affects the quality of the honey product. Understanding this concern among customers, in this article Mother Nature will introduce readers to the top 5 **_high-quality honey_** brands in Vietnam, so customers can choose and use them with peace of mind.

## Introducing Hoa Ban Food Honey

Four years of continuously harvesting liters of pure honey from the old-growth forests at the upper reaches of the Da River in the Northwest, Hoa Ban Food has won no small amount of affection and admiration from customers. Renowned for its 100% genuine pure wild honey, every trip means climbing up to harvest honey from wild bee hives. Not only trekking through mountains and streams, Hoa Ban Food has also ventured into remote villages to buy up liters of honey from ethnic minority communities such as the Thai, La Hu, and Ha Nhi people. With such painstaking investment, Hoa Ban Food has earned the support of many customers throughout Vietnam. Hoa Ban Food's website: [hoabanfood.com](https://hoabanfood.com/)

## Mother Nature Honey – A Gift from Mother Nature

Mother Nature is a **_high-quality honey_** brand well known in the market thanks to a long tradition of beekeeping. Originating from a beekeeping household in Ba Ria, Mother Nature has built a solid depth of experience in harvesting and processing honey. The connection among beekeeping households in the region gives Mother Nature access to an abundant, pure supply of natural honey, ensuring quality for its customers. Not only that, Mother Nature also applies science and technology to create convenient, easy-to-drink processed honey products such as lemon ginger honey and royal jelly honey, meeting the increasingly high and discerning demands of the market.

Mother Nature's website: [mothernature.vn](https://monabee.vn/)

## Co Cu Honey

Co Cu Honey is the result of a dedicated 5-year journey by two brothers, Duc Minh and Duc Duy. Their large-scale farm, with more than 5,500 beehives, travels across the country to take advantage of flowering seasons to produce honey. Because of this, Co Cu honey always has a distinctive flavor, with the two brothers finding it most wonderful to bring customers nature's finest **_high-quality honey_** products.

## Cat Ba Honey from Cat Ba National Park

This is a well-known **_high-quality honey_** brand widely featured, especially on Vietnamese travel service websites, and highly rated by experts for its flavor and quality. At Cat Ba National Park, there's a specialty that no visitor can forget: pure wild forest honey. This honey is harvested from one of the three rarest species of bees at Cat Ba National Park - the "native" bee. To produce such delicious, high-quality liters of wild honey, since the 1990s local residents of Cat Ba have applied a very smart process for raising wild bees, letting the colonies out to gather nectar from the various flowers in Cat Ba National Park.

## The Mint Flower Honey Brand from Bac Giang

This type of honey is favored by customers for its rich sweetness and distinctively soothing aroma. It has beneficial effects for treating coughs and nourishing gut health. This honey comes from mint flower nectar, a type of flower that grows naturally and wild in the rocky terrain of Ha Giang. Thanks to this natural origin, mint flower honey is trusted by many people.
