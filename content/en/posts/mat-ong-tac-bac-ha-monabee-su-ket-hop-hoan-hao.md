### Kumquat

Kumquat is a tropical fruit rich in vitamin C and other antioxidants. It's an essential ingredient for cleansing the body and boosting resistance.

**Benefits of kumquat:**

  * **Rich in vitamin C:** Kumquat has a very high vitamin C content, which helps the body produce collagen, keeping skin firm and healthy. Vitamin C also helps fight free radicals, protecting the body from illness and inflammation.
  * **Boosts the immune system:** The combination of vitamin C and flavonoids in kumquat helps strengthen disease prevention, especially for respiratory illnesses such as flu and sore throat.
  * **Supports digestion:** Kumquat juice helps stimulate the secretion of gastric juices and digestive enzymes, supporting more effective digestion.

### Mint

Mint is a widely used herb known for its cooling effect, stress relief, and respiratory support. Mint contains menthol, a compound that delivers a refreshing sensation, soothes the respiratory tract, and improves blood circulation.

**Benefits of mint:**

  * **Reduces coughing and sore throat:** The menthol in mint soothes the throat, reduces coughing, and helps clear the airways, easing symptoms related to colds and sore throat.
  * **Relaxes and relieves stress:** Mint's cool, refreshing scent helps the body relax, reducing stress and fatigue. Regular use of mint can also help improve sleep.
  * **Stimulates digestion:** Mint supports digestion, reduces bloating and indigestion, and helps regulate digestive function.
