Many women today face various skin issues at every age... a multi-step skincare routine that still hasn't improved their skin. This article will reveal how to beautify your facial skin with honey and ingredients readily available at home, in an incredibly simple way.

 

[****Honey****](https://monabee.vn/) **is not just simply a "medicine" from nature that's good for health, but also has many other uses as an ingredient for skincare, treating acne, and effectively fighting aging.**

Honey is considered a natural miracle remedy that's very good for health and skin. Most honey contains a large amount of potassium, magnesium, and calcium that help regenerate and restore the skin, support anti-inflammatory action, prevent acne, and fade dark spots—which is why this ingredient is often used to make skincare masks.

![](/images/posts/cong-dung-cua-mat-na-mat-ong-danh-bay-vet-tham-da/11.jpg)

__Honey is considered a natural miracle remedy that's very good for health and skin__

____

****Honey and yogurt mask****

Using this skincare mask made from honey and unsweetened yogurt not only effectively whitens the skin, it also provides optimal moisture, prevents wrinkles, and makes skin brighter and firmer.

How to make it:

– Take 2 spoons of honey and mix with 2 spoons of plain unsweetened yogurt, mixing thoroughly until the two ingredients are fully combined.

****Honey and egg mask****

Eggs contain a large amount of protein, so the combination of honey and egg creates a wonderful skincare mask mixture for women with rough, less firm skin. This honey and egg mask helps restore skin that shows signs of aging, firms the skin, and shrinks pores.

____

__![](/images/posts/cong-dung-cua-mat-na-mat-ong-danh-bay-vet-tham-da/111.jpg)__

__This honey and egg mask helps restore skin and fight aging__

____

How to make it:

Mix 1 egg white + 1 spoon of pure honey, whisk the mixture evenly, then massage it evenly onto your skin. Leave it on for about 20 minutes, then wash your face with warm water.

[****Honey and turmeric mask****](https://monabee.vn/san-pham/mat-ong-nguyen-chat-ngam-nghe-hu-180ml/)

[**Turmeric**](https://www.vinmec.com/vi/tin-tuc/thong-tin-suc-khoe/lam-dep/nghe-co-tac-dung-lam-dep-da-khong/) contains many natural compounds, especially a large amount of curcumin. These substances can brighten the skin and help it look firmer and more radiant. In addition, turmeric also helps restore skin by giving it a naturally brighter tone.

![](/images/posts/cong-dung-cua-mat-na-mat-ong-danh-bay-vet-tham-da/nghệ.jpg)

____This honey and turmeric mask helps fade scars and dark spots and gives you naturally beautiful skin__

____

____How to make it:

– Mix 30g of turmeric powder, 2 teaspoons of honey, and ¼ container of unsweetened yogurt into a thick paste, then refrigerate for about 5 minutes.

– Wash your face with warm water, then apply the mixture directly to your skin.

– Once the mask has dried (about 10-15 minutes), rinse it off with cold water.
