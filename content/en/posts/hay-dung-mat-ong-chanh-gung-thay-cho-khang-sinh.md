## What is antibiotic resistance?

Children are especially sensitive to weather changes — when it suddenly turns cold, children often come down with respiratory illnesses, with common symptoms like a stuffy nose, sneezing, and a runny nose. What's worth noting here is that parents often buy antibiotics on their own to give to their children, without following any doctor's guidance. Frequent use like this can easily cause antibiotic resistance in children, contributing to the spread and growth of drug-resistant bacteria in the community.
