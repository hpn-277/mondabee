No need to say too much about the benefits of honey - Mother Nature already has an entire article discussing honey's health benefits. In this article, Mother Nature will share a few ways to **_prepare honey_** with other foods and ingredients to make some truly appealing dishes and drinks.

## How to Mix Honey with Lemon

To cleanse the body and treat common respiratory ailments like a dry cough, cold, or sore throat, a cup of warm water mixed with lemon is certainly a wonderful remedy. A mixture of warm water with honey and lemon also works tirelessly to cleanse and detoxify the liver, purify the body, and strengthen the immune system. Using it regularly will help your body get the vitamins and minerals it needs. As for the mixing ratio, the recommended proportion of honey to lemon juice is 2:1. You can adjust the amount of warm water to suit your taste.

## How to Mix Honey and Ginger

The way to **_prepare honey_** with ginger is a little different from honey with lemon. First, you need to boil water and add thinly sliced ginger to it. Don't forget to cover the pot and simmer on low heat for 5 to 10 minutes. Then, wait for the water to cool slightly before stirring in honey and enjoying; if you don't like eating the ginger itself, you can strain it out. This mixture is especially good for treating muscle aches, boosting digestive function, and reducing symptoms of menstrual cramps and migraines.

## Cooking Honey with Other Dishes

Honey is a rather special type of liquid, containing only about 20% water, yet in terms of sweetness it far surpasses other types of sugar. So, when cooking and **_preparing honey_** with other foods, you need to pay attention to seasoning so the dish doesn't turn out too sweet. In grilled and roasted dishes, honey is the main seasoning for marinating things like grilled pork ribs, roast duck, and grilled chicken, because honey has a light sweetness that, when brushed onto these foods, gives them a delicious flavor and an irresistibly glossy golden color. One thing to keep in mind when cooking dishes marinated with honey is that honey burns easily, so you need to watch closely and lower the heat compared to dishes cooked with sugar. Honey is a perfect substitute for sugar for those who prefer it. Along with various detox drinks and fruit juices, honey also naturally enhances the flavor in its own distinctive way.

## Don't Cook Honey with Foods It Doesn't Mix With

Although honey is an ingredient that easily combines and cooks well with other ingredients, there are still a few cases you need to be careful about if you don't want to risk poisoning, harm to your health, or worse, danger to your life. Honey strongly clashes with two familiar Vietnamese cooking ingredients: carp and dill - you should absolutely never cook honey with these ingredients.
