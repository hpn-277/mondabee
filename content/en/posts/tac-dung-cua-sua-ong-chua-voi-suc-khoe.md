> [**Royal jelly**](https://hellobacsi.com/an-uong-lanh-manh/thong-tin-dinh-duong/ban-da-biet-phan-biet-sua-ong-chua-va-mat-ong-hay-chua/) is a gelatin-like substance secreted by honeybees to feed the queen bee and her larvae. At room temperature, royal jelly resembles butter, pale in color with a slight yellow tinge, and is especially nutritious. Royal jelly is stored in a special cell used only for the queen bee or bees that are about to develop into queens.

Thanks to its special nutrient content, royal jelly also helps support the treatment of some common ailments, helps beautify skin, and prevents aging. Fresh royal jelly contains water, carbohydrates, protein, and fat.

[_**Royal jelly**_](https://hellobacsi.com/an-uong-lanh-manh/thong-tin-dinh-duong/ban-da-biet-phan-biet-sua-ong-chua-va-mat-ong-hay-chua/) also contains some B vitamins and trace minerals. The components of royal jelly have not yet been fully verified, but its health benefits mainly come from its special proteins and fatty acids.

![](/images/posts/tac-dung-cua-sua-ong-chua-voi-suc-khoe/sua-ong-chua-la-gi-tac-dung-cua-sua-ong-chua-2.jpg)

The _**benefits of royal jelly**_ have been proven by many scientific researchers. The amino acids, fatty acids, and phenolic compounds in royal jelly have strong antioxidant properties that help prevent signs of premature skin aging such as wrinkles, dark spots, and hidden acne.

Not only that, royal jelly also helps prevent macular degeneration and other chronic diseases that appear at various ages.  
Royal jelly reduces the risk of heart disease

* **Royal Jelly** lowers blood sugar:  
Royal jelly helps protect the heart and circulatory system by lowering blood pressure. The special proteins in royal jelly relax the smooth muscle cells in veins and arteries, thereby lowering blood pressure. Royal jelly combined with other bee-derived substances also helps significantly reduce blood pressure.  
* **Royal Jelly** regulates blood sugar:  
The benefits of royal jelly include helping control blood sugar levels and insulin sensitivity by reducing oxidative imbalance and inflammation. Many studies show increased insulin sensitivity and enhanced protective effects on pancreatic, liver, and reproductive tissue in people with obesity or diabetes who use royal jelly.

 

![](/images/posts/tac-dung-cua-sua-ong-chua-voi-suc-khoe/sữa-ong-chúa-1.jpg)

**– Royal jelly** can help heal wounds and some other skin inflammation conditions.

As a result, the benefits of royal jelly help fight bacteria, keeping wounds clean and free of infection. This is because royal jelly promotes increased collagen production, a protein that plays an important structural role in repairing skin damage.

  * **Who should use royal jelly** :  
People with sexual weakness, infertility, insomnia, memory decline, those in perimenopause and menopause, people with high blood pressure, high blood lipids, hair loss or baldness, malnourished children, and those with acne, melasma, freckles, rheumatism, arthritis, etc.
  * **Who should not use royal jelly** :  
You should not use royal jelly if you have low blood pressure, low blood sugar, diarrhea, an infectious disease or fever, are pregnant, are a child under 13 years old, have sensitive skin prone to pollen allergies, or are undergoing treatment for breast cancer.



With its wealth of important nutrients, royal jelly is a necessary supplementary source of nutrition for the body.

To get the best out of royal jelly's benefits, you should thoroughly research how to store and use it properly to avoid any unwanted side effects.
