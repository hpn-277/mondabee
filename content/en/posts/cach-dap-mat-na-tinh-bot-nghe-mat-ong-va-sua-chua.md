### 

### ****[Turmeric powder, honey, and unsweetened yogurt face mask](https://monabee.vn/san-pham/hop-qua-cao-cap-mat-nhan-sam-tinh-bot-nghe/)****

### The **turmeric powder, honey, and unsweetened yogurt face mask** has disinfecting, acne-fighting, dark-spot-reducing, scar-healing, and anti-aging effects, while also exfoliating dead skin cells, moisturizing the skin, and effectively softening and smoothing it.

### _Prepare the following tools and ingredients_:

  * ### 1 small bowl to make the mask

  * ### 1 small dish to hold the mask mixture

  * ### 2 spoons of honey (pure honey is best)

  * ### 2 spoons of turmeric powder

  * ### 1 container of unsweetened yogurt




### 

### Method:

  * ### After you've prepared all the ingredients as instructed, put the pure honey, turmeric powder, and yogurt into a bowl and mix well into a soft, smooth mixture. Transfer the mixture into a small dish and keep it in the fridge for later use, but only use this mixture within 1 week.

  * ### Wash your face with warm water and gently massage to remove dead skin cells and open up your pores, helping your skin absorb nutrients better before applying the mixture to your face. Then let your face dry naturally.

  * ### Apply the mask mixture to your face, gently massaging in circular motions for 3-5 minutes so the nutrients penetrate deep into the skin, then relax and lie down for about 15-20 minutes.

  * ### Finally, rinse your face with cold water, as cold water will help tighten your pores.




### It's best to apply the mask in the evening before bed, since your skin won't be exposed to sunlight or pollution at that time. Use the mask 2-3 times a week, and after 4 consecutive weeks you'll have bright, rosy, smooth, and healthy skin.
