## 1. Monabee honey is standard-grade honey

One of the most important factors in evaluating honey quality is its origin and the beekeeping process. Monabee honey is farmed honey produced through a closed-loop process, strictly complying with international honey standards. Thanks to a natural beekeeping environment and modern bee-care techniques, Monabee honey retains its pure, nutrient-rich quality on par with wild forest honey.

### a. C4 sugar index close to zero

The C4 sugar index is one of the important criteria for determining honey's origin. Honey containing C4 sugar is a sign that the bees consumed cane sugar or other industrial sugars during honey production, rather than gathering nectar from flowers.

This greatly affects honey quality. However, Monabee honey has a C4 sugar index of approximately zero, similar to natural wild forest honey. This proves that Monabee honey is not adulterated and that the bees gathered nectar entirely from flowers, ensuring maximum purity.

### b. Produced through a closed-loop process meeting export standards

### a. What is freeze-drying technology?

**Freeze-drying technology** is a modern drying process in which the product is cooled to a very low temperature (usually -40°C), after which the water in the product sublimates directly from a solid to a vapor state, without going through a liquid phase. This technology preserves the flavor, color, and nutrients of the product.

### b. Applying freeze-drying to Monabee honey

Monabee applies freeze-drying to **natural herbs** such as ginger, lemon, turmeric, and kumquat to create its special herbal honey products. The freeze-drying process fully preserves the beneficial active compounds in the herbs, ensuring users receive the highest quality product:
