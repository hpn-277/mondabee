## Monabee at the Mega Live "TONE OF AGRICULTURE"

Mega Live "TONE OF AGRICULTURE" is a special event bringing together fresh, quality agricultural products from well-known brands. This was not only an occasion for brands to showcase their outstanding products, but also an opportunity for consumers to directly experience fresh agricultural products, especially Monabee honey.  
Coming to the event, Monabee was proud to stand alongside other quality agricultural brands, bringing customers wonderful experiences with pure, naturally ripened honey combined with freeze-dried authentic Vietnamese herbs, rich in nutrients and good for health. Monabee's presence at the event became even more meaningful as it was an occasion for consumers to better understand the value of quality agricultural products, an ideal choice for health and life.

## Le Duong Bao Lam – Monabee's Companion at Mega Live

One of the special moments of the Mega Live "TONE OF AGRICULTURE" was the participation of Le Duong Bao Lam – a KOL well known in the online and media community. With his charm, humor, and enthusiasm, Le Duong Bao Lam made the livestream more lively, engaging, and relatable than ever.  
Sharing his experience with Monabee honey products, Le Duong Bao Lam not only praised the quality of the honey but also affirmed his fondness for quality agricultural products. Monabee honey, he said, is a product every family should have, as it not only brings a naturally sweet flavor but is also good for health. This was a strong affirmation of the quality and value of Monabee's products.
