Honey is a natural sweetener that humans have used for thousands of years. But it's not simply a natural sugar - honey contains many nutrients beneficial to health.

**_Health Benefits of Honey:_**

  * **Antibacterial and anti-inflammatory:** Thanks to its natural antibacterial compounds, honey helps kill harmful bacteria while reducing inflammation and soothing damage to the skin and body. This helps improve infections and speed up wound healing.

Turmeric is a precious herb in traditional medicine, known for its wound-healing, anti-inflammatory, and powerful antioxidant properties. In Monabee's turmeric royal jelly honey product, turmeric is used in freeze-dried form, preserving all of its precious nutrients and active compounds.

**_Curcumin – The Main Component of Turmeric:_**

Curcumin is a powerful bioactive compound in turmeric with anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and even anti-cancer effects. Curcumin has been extensively studied and shown to reduce inflammation and lower the risk of chronic diseases such as heart disease, cancer, and Alzheimer's disease.

**_Health Benefits of Turmeric:_**

  * **Supports digestion:** Turmeric stimulates the secretion of bile, thereby improving digestion and fat absorption. This is especially important for people suffering from indigestion, bloating, or gastritis.

### **Royal Jelly**

Royal jelly is a substance secreted from the pharyngeal glands of worker bees, used to feed bee larvae and the queen bee. It is rich in proteins, amino acids, B vitamins, minerals, and powerful antioxidant compounds.
