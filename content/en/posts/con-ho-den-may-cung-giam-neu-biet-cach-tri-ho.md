Using antibiotics to treat coughs in children will negatively affect their resistance in the long run. Soaking honey with kumquat is trusted by mothers. However, not everyone knows how to properly prepare a nutritious jar of honey kumquat. Today, Ong Tam Dao will guide you on **how to treat your child's cough with honey and kumquat, extremely effectively**.

## **Guide: how to treat your child's cough with honey and kumquat**

Honey and kumquat are considered natural, gentle antibiotics that help fight respiratory illnesses. Let's learn how to **[treat your child's cough with honey and kumquat](https://monabee.vn/2019/12/05/con-ho-den-may-cung-giam-neu-biet-cach-tri-ho-bang-mat-ong-quat/)** below:

### **Ingredients needed**

To **treat your child's cough with honey and kumquat**, you need to prepare the following ingredients:

  * 200g kumquat
  * [150ml honey](https://monabee.vn/san-pham/mat-ong-nguyen-chat-hu-thuy-tinh-180ml/)
  * A little white salt
  * 1 glass jar



### **Guide to effectively soaking honey with kumquat**

The ingredients you need are as follows:

Step 1: First, in [**this method of treating your child's cough with honey and kumquat**](https://monabee.vn/2019/12/05/con-ho-den-may-cung-giam-neu-biet-cach-tri-ho-bang-mat-ong-quat/), soak the kumquats you've bought in dilute salt water to remove bacteria. Then drain them thoroughly and use clean paper to blot up any remaining water on the fruit.

![Treating your child's cough with honey and kumquat](http://honeco.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/quat-bo-doi.jpg)Kumquats cut in half with seeds removed

Step 2: Next, cut the kumquats in half or into thin slices and remove the seeds. Layer the kumquat into the glass jar, alternating a layer of kumquat with a layer of honey, until you've used all the kumquat.

![Guide to treating your child's cough with kumquat and honey](http://honeco.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/quat-ngam-mat-ong.jpg)Layer the kumquat into the glass jar, alternating a layer of kumquat with a layer of honey

Step 3: Seal the lid tightly and store in a dry, well-ventilated place; it can be used after about 3 days.

### **Guide to using honey-soaked kumquat to treat your child's cough**

  * For young children: Each time, take 2–3 spoons of honey kumquat mixed with warm water, and drink in the morning before eating and in the evening before bed.
  * For older children: Besides drinking it mixed with warm water, you can give them pieces of kumquat soaked in honey to suck on, then chew and swallow.
  * For adults: Suck on the kumquat and occasionally drink a bit of the honey-soaked kumquat liquid.



Making a jar of honey kumquat to treat your child's cough takes about 3 days before it's ready to use. If you don't have any on hand and want to treat your child's cough right away, you can **make steamed honey kumquat.**

### **Guide to making steamed honey kumquat to treat your child's cough**

Steaming honey with kumquat effectively reduces coughing, sore throat, throat irritation, and hoarseness. Let's explore [**how to steam honey with kumquat**](https://monabee.vn/2019/12/05/con-ho-den-may-cung-giam-neu-biet-cach-tri-ho-bang-mat-ong-quat/) with Ong Tam Dao!

Ingredients: 3 green kumquats, 3 spoons of honey, 1 small bowl, a plate

How to make it: Wash the kumquats with salt water, cut them in half, and keep the seeds intact. If steaming, you don't need to remove the seeds—kumquat seeds contain a compound that helps loosen phlegm and warms the throat. Next, put the cut kumquat and honey into a small, deep dish. Then steam the mixture over a water bath for about 20 to 30 minutes.

How to use it: After steaming the honey kumquat, wait until it cools and give it to your child, ideally while still warm. Give your child the mixture about 3 times a day, and the cough will ease. For older children, you can give them a piece of kumquat and have them chew and swallow it.

## **Important notes when treating your child's cough with honey and kumquat**

This method isn't just suitable for young children—every family member can use it. Honey kumquat helps boost resistance and effectively prevents respiratory illnesses. Below are some notes to keep in mind when **treating your child's cough with honey and kumquat**

### **How long does honey-soaked kumquat keep?**

Don't use honey-soaked kumquat if it has an unusual smell. After opening the lid, be sure to close it tightly again—letting in air will cause the honey kumquat to spoil more quickly. Store it in a dry, well-ventilated place away from direct sunlight.

### **Does honey-soaked kumquat have an expiration date?**

Honey can be kept for under 18 months.

### **Can honey-soaked kumquat treat coughs in newborns?**

Honey should not be given to children under 1 year old, so honey-soaked kumquat cannot be used to treat coughs in newborns. Instead of honey, use rock sugar. Steamed kumquat with rock sugar, or steamed lemon basil leaves with rock sugar, are excellent remedies for treating coughs in newborns.

![](http://honeco.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/khong-nen-chua-ho-bang-mat-ong-quat-cho-tre-so-sinh.jpg)Do not treat coughs in newborns with honey-soaked kumquat

### **Notes when treating your child's cough with honey and kumquat**

  * Medical research has proven that honey should not be used for children under 1 year old.
  * When soaking honey with kumquat, use a glass jar that has been thoroughly sterilized and dried, so the honey kumquat doesn't spoil.
  * When using honey kumquat to treat your child's cough, pay special attention to sourcing clean ingredients—cleanliness is the first standard for making a remedy.
