## What is Covid-19 treatment medication?

The two Covid-19 treatment drugs currently on the market are Paxlovid, made by [Pfizer](https://vietnamese.cdc.gov/coronavirus/2019-ncov/vaccines/different-vaccines/Pfizer-BioNTech.html), and Molnupiravir, made by Merck. Even though these are drugs meant to treat Covid, keep in mind that they are not prescribed to everyone who tests positive for SARS-CoV-2 — only to those with mild to moderate illness. Patients with severe cases, the elderly, or those with chronic conditions such as heart disease, lung disease, diabetes, or obesity should not use these drugs and instead need to be hospitalized for treatment.

## Things to note when using Covid-19 treatment medication

Important things you need to remember when using Covid-19 treatment medication:

  * Treatment should last for 5 days
  * With Paxlovid, you take 3 pills at once, twice a day
  * With Molnupiravir, you take 4 pills, twice a day
  * You must follow the doctor's and medical staff's instructions strictly — taking it incorrectly will affect the treatment's effectiveness
  * Paxlovid is limited to those aged 12 and up, while Molnupiravir is limited to those aged 18 and up
  * Pregnant women must absolutely not use Molnupiravir, as it may have adverse effects on the fetus due to potential risks of birth defects. Men who use the drug should avoid conceiving a child for 3 months after use, since the drug's effects on sperm are not yet fully understood
  * Both drugs can interact with certain other medications, so users should be careful to avoid drug interactions

Therefore, before using Covid-19 treatment medication, you should carefully consult a doctor or medical staff.
