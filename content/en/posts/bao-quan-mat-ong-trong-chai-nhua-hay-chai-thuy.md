# How long can honey be stored?

Honey is a natural food that's extremely good for our bodies. With its excellent nutrient content and medicinal value, this food has long been studied and used as a remedy throughout the past.

Alongside honey's health benefits, there have also been many discoveries about this special food's [storage](https://monabee.vn/2024/07/09/bao-quan-mat-ong-trong-chai-nhua-hay-chai-thuy-tinh-thi-tot/) life and use.

The oldest known batch of honey was excavated in Georgia and is estimated to be 5,000 years old. Scientists who studied this honey concluded that it was still edible.

Herodotus reported that the ancient Babylonians buried their dead in honey. The embalming method using honey was also applied to Alexander the Great. And in 1913, American archaeologists discovered a jar of honey in an Egyptian pyramid tomb determined to be over 3,000 years old. Most surprising of all, the honey in the jar had not spoiled at all and was still edible, retaining its original delicious flavor.

Many questions arise about honey's shelf life: Does honey have an expiration date? How long can honey be kept?

Almost all packaged foods we use daily have a shelf life. Still, if not stored properly, they can spoil sooner than usual.

Of course, there are also foods that can last several years, several decades, or even several hundred years. Honey, salt, and wine are prime examples.

Amina Harris, Executive Director of the Honey and Pollination Center at the Robert Mondavi Institute at the University of California, says, "Honey in its natural form has very low moisture. When bacteria and microorganisms fall into it, all they find is dead sugar. Essentially, food spoils because of microorganisms and bacteria. When they cannot survive in honey, they have no chance to spoil it."

Another thing that sets honey apart from ordinary sugars is its acidity. Honey's pH is around 3 to 4.5. Because of this acidic environment, microorganisms are destroyed and cannot survive in pure honey.

On the other hand, honey itself has antibacterial properties that can inhibit the reproduction and growth of microorganisms.

# Is it better to store honey in a plastic bottle or a glass bottle?

Based on the research above, honey is classified as a food with a very long shelf life if stored properly. The key to storing this food is to keep it in a dry place, tightly sealed, and away from water and direct sunlight. That's why the container used to hold honey is extremely important.

There are currently two types of bottles commonly used to hold and store honey: plastic bottles and glass bottles. So which one is actually better?

## 

## **Storing honey in plastic bottles**

Many brands choose plastic bottles to hold honey because they're cheap and come in many sizes and shapes. Moreover, plastic bottles have better flexibility than glass bottles, which is an advantage for holding honey. This is because honey can generate air bubbles during transport that could crack a glass bottle.

Plastic bottles are also lightweight and convenient, which is why they're favored. Still, plastic materials have never been recommended for everyday use. That's because microplastic particles carry many potential health risks. When they break down, they release harmful toxins that affect and weaken the immune system, causing dangerous illnesses such as cancer. Microplastics can also persist in the environment for hundreds or even thousands of years without decomposing. This not only causes pollution but also does tremendous harm to the Earth's ecosystem.

Many people use PET bottles to hold honey. This is a fairly safe flexible plastic, but it can only be used once and cannot be recycled. Worse, this type of plastic oxidizes very easily in air and can easily release toxic substances at high temperatures.

## 

## **Storing honey in glass bottles**

Compared to plastic, honey stored in glass bottles/jars looks more appealing—the honey looks more delicious, and you can clearly see its natural color and viscosity. In addition, glass is an inert material that doesn't change or produce toxic substances during use. So you can rest completely assured about its safety for health, as well as reuse it many times.

Choosing a quality glass bottle/jar with good thickness and durability will reduce the risk of breakage during transport. In addition, there are now many honey bottles available in different shapes and sizes to suit users' needs.

Because they can be reused many times, glass bottles are also more environmentally friendly than plastic ones. Storing honey in a glass jar is the optimal choice for health, the environment, and ensuring the highest quality of the honey.

One more note when storing honey: the honey itself must be pure, free of impurities, and naturally ripened in the hive, because in that case its water content is reduced to the minimum, allowing the honey to preserve its nutrients and flavor for a long time.
