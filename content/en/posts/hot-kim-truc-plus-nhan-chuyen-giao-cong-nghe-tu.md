## Introducing Kim Truc Plus

Kim Truc Plus was founded with the goal of changing Vietnamese people's perception of domestic honey brands. Thanks to its distinctive climate and natural blessings — abundant melaleuca forests and coffee plantations — honey from Ba Ria-Vung Tau has a wonderful flavor, beautiful color, and high quality. Ms. Thanh Truc, co-founder of Kim Truc Plus, has always nurtured the passion to take Ba Ria-Vung Tau's natural honey to greater heights. Kim Truc Plus's flagship products include the natural honey brand Mother Nature, with honey with lemon and ginger, honey with royal jelly, and honey with ginseng. Mother Nature honey is now available at its showroom and through online channels such as its website, Tiki, Lazada, and Shopee.

## What is the technology transfer from the BRVT Provincial Industrial Promotion Center?

Ba Ria-Vung Tau province issued an Industrial Promotion Program to mobilize resources supporting organizations and individuals investing in the province's industrial and handicraft production, with the aim of creating jobs, increasing income, and stabilizing the lives of rural workers. At the same time, it encourages the transfer and use of science and technology for cleaner rural production, improving the efficiency of using natural resources, raw materials, fuel, and materials. It reduces emissions and limits environmental pollution to protect human health and the surrounding living environment, all working toward the ultimate goal of green growth and sustainable development over time. Through this, the program supports and creates favorable conditions for organizations and individuals across all economic sectors to invest in sustainable industrial and handicraft production development, boost competitiveness, and effectively carry out the roadmap for integration into the broader international economy.

## Kim Truc Plus is honored to receive the technology transfer

Recently, Kim Truc Plus was honored to receive a technology transfer from the Ba Ria-Vung Tau Provincial Industrial Promotion Center, holding an equipment acceptance ceremony under the national incentive program. It's worth noting that, during this period, only 2 companies in Ba Ria-Vung Tau province received this program. This is a great honor for Kim Truc Plus and a solid step forward in bringing the Mother Nature honey brand to customers across the country.
