## The Largest Agricultural Mega Live Event in Central Vietnam

The Largest Agricultural Mega Live in Central Vietnam is a large-scale event held to honor and promote high-quality Vietnamese agricultural products. The livestream not only introduced Hue's specialty products but also featured hundreds of brands from all 63 provinces and cities. The event was organized through a collaboration between reputable organizations such as FoodMap, TikTok Shop, the Thua Thien Hue Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, the Thua Thien Hue Young Entrepreneurs Association, the Vietnam E-commerce Association, Vietnam Post, Maybe, CER GROUP, Quang Linh Vlog, and Hang Du Muc Entertainment. The event was also part of National E-commerce Week and Vietnam Online Shopping Day – Online Friday 2024, opening up major opportunities for Vietnamese agricultural products to connect with consumers both domestically and abroad.  
This was an occasion for Vietnamese agricultural products to be honored and introduced to the consumer community, while also building a bridge between businesses and customers from all regions. A diverse range of agricultural products was present at the event, from Hue's famous specialties to representative goods from other provinces. Among them, Monabee stood out as one of the notable brands with unique products combining pure honey with authentic Vietnamese herbs.

## Monabee – Honored to Take Part

Monabee is a leading brand in Vietnam's honey and natural product manufacturing industry. Monabee's products are made from pure coffee-flower honey combined with distinctive herbs such as ginger, kumquat, and lemon, bringing excellent health benefits to consumers. Monabee's participation in the Largest Agricultural Mega Live in Central Vietnam was not only an opportunity to promote its products, but also a chance to affirm its position in Vietnam's agricultural industry.

Monabee's standout product at this event was lemon ginger honey. Lemon ginger honey is a wonderful combination of naturally ripened pure honey with sliced native ginger and paper lemon, freeze-dried to help reduce coughs and sore throats, support digestion, and naturally boost the body's resistance. The product is made using modern freeze-drying technology, which preserves the natural color and flavor, and in particular retains up to 99.9% of the nutritional content and medicinal properties inherent in the ingredients.  
Monabee's products have been highly rated, not only for their outstanding quality but also for their protective and health-caring features for consumers. Monabee has established its name with a clear point of difference, offering products that are friendly, easy to use, and good for health.
