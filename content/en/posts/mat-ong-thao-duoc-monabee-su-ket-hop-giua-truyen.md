## I. Freeze-Drying Technology in Monabee Herbal Honey

### 1. What Is Freeze-Drying Technology?

**Freeze-drying** is one of the most advanced technologies today for preserving food and medicinal ingredients. This process involves two main steps:

  * **Rapidly freezing** the herbal ingredients.
  * Then, under a **vacuum** environment, the water in the ingredients evaporates directly from a solid to a vapor state, without passing through a liquid phase.

Thanks to this process, the herbs retain their entire structure, flavor, and especially their precious **nutrients**, without being altered by high temperatures. This helps Monabee herbal honey **retain its medicinal properties** and deliver **outstanding effectiveness** when used.

### 2. Advantages of Freeze-Drying Technology in Monabee Herbal Honey

  * **Preserves Active Compounds**: Unlike traditional heat-drying methods, freeze-drying technology preserves the important active compounds in herbs, increasing their effectiveness when combined with honey.
  * **Natural Flavor**: The herbs retain their natural flavor after drying, giving Monabee honey a delicious, easy-to-drink taste.
  * **Long-Term Storage Capability**: Freeze-dried products can be stored for a long time without needing chemical preservatives, making them safe for users.
